# Predictions auxiliary-state cutover: run sheet (proposal for owner review, nothing is authorized by this page)

This is the minute-by-minute sequence of the bounded L3 operation behind [ADR 0194](../adr/0194-prediction-auxiliary-state-d1-checkpoint-and-outbox.md) and [ADR 0196](../adr/0196-prediction-aux-admission-closed-state.md). It describes who does what, what is read before each step and when to stop. **Every row marked GO needs its own explicit owner decision; none is granted by this page.** The commands are those of [the operator](prediction-aux-state-cutover.md#the-owner-only-cutover-operator-dormant-nothing-here-is-authorized-by-merging-it) and [the export runbook](r2-snapshot-export.md). The live readback behind the gates is in the [readiness evidence](../evidence/prediction/air-554-cutover-readiness-readback-2026-11-22.md).

Actors: **Owner** (GO, flip release, extensions), **Coordinator** (the Predictions coordinator: Task registry snapshot, disable and enable, and nothing else), **Migration** (the executor: schema, marker, export, import, verification, integrity evidence). Times are minutes from **T0**, the moment the coordinator reads back four of four Tasks disabled; the checkpoint and the limit are anchored at T0. Steps after the close are timed from **C**, the readback of the closed marker, and C is reached when the drain is verified, not at a fixed minute. `T-` rows are done before the window and outside any freeze.

## Scope contract of the scoped export (implemented offline, ADR 0200; used only if the owner selects it at P4)

`node scripts/r2-snapshot-export.mjs --scope prediction-aux` (plan, export and `--verify` all take it; `--verify` names the scope the snapshot carries and refuses another) proves consistency over **one domain**, and the converter, the operator and the release carry the proof:

- **Scope:** the exact keys `enrichment/batch-stage.json` and `enrichment/event-outbox.json` and the complete prefixes `enrichment/sources/`, `enrichment/batch-manifest/`, `enrichment/stop-events/` and `enrichment/start-events/`, defined once in `scripts/prediction-aux-scope.mjs` and tied by a test to the importer's families and the Worker's closed gate. A key under a prefix that the importer would not consume stays in the cut and is refused by the converter.
- **Proof:** opening and closing inventories of exactly that scope (a complete listing of each prefix and a `HEAD` of each exact key), a conditional `HEAD`, `GET` and `HEAD` of every object with its SHA-256, and the scope's own fence: the batch runner and writer leases. Any object added, removed or changed inside the scope, or a lease change, is a NO-GO. The EPG and Discovery coordination keys and every other object are not listed, not read and not compared; unrelated writers cannot change the verdict.
- **Provenance:** the manifest and the converter report record the scope (`kind`, `digest`); the operator accepts only two captures that carry the same proof, refuses an unsupported digest, and the release binds the proof kind. An output directory is never reused across scopes. Old whole-bucket snapshots stay valid.
- **What it does not claim:** atomicity with D1, any property of unrelated objects (they are not migrated data), or a PUT that lands after the closing inventory (the residual of ADR 0196). The exporter has no D1 access; the closed marker is checked by the operator at the flip, and a legitimate write between the first cut and the close changes the second cut and refuses the flip.

The whole-bucket mode is unchanged and remains the fallback only with the quiet-writer condition of P4.

## Stop and recovery

- **Stop beats the timer.** The checkpoint is T+30; the planned maximum is T+45 from the disable readback. Silence of a Task, an equal listing or a green status is never proof of a drained writer.
- **Before the first import statement:** `--reopen` (`closed` to `r2` at G+2) is allowed only while the auxiliary tables are empty; after it the coordinator enables the four Tasks with only `is_enabled:true`. Marker and rows must be read back first.
- **After an import exists:** `--reopen` is refused by design and the flip is irreversible. If time runs out or marker and rows are unclear: the marker stays `closed`, the Tasks stay disabled, no blind rollback, no forced unfreeze, the owner decides. A restore from the snapshot is not designed here and would be its own approved operation.
- **Uncertain write:** the operator reports `write_uncertain`, reads back, never retries; the next step is read-only until the owner decides.
- **Code rollback** does not revert the marker or the data: a Worker older than the `closed` gate must not run against a marker at `closed` or `d1`.
- The residual of ADR 0196 stands: a PUT that passed its marker read and lands after the final capture is not seen by any listing and cannot be recalled.

## What this page does not authorize

The export, the credential, `0034`, the Task disable, the close, the import, the flip, any Workflow or flag change and any manual run each need their own owner decision. Merging this page records a proposal.
