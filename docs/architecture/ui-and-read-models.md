# UI and read-model projections

This diagram describes the **current read projections**: D1 is authoritative per domain, and R2 contributes only the roles drawn (immutable version bodies). The view index is not an R2 role any more: it is always derived from the D1 rows and the retired raw object is neither read nor written ([view index contract](../reference/domain/view-index-read-model.md), [ADR 0192](../adr/0192-retire-view-index-selector-and-raw-object.md)). Compatibility mirrors such as `programme.json`, `watchlist.json` and `metadata.json` are not read as authority and are not drawn. It is not a new canonical Film database. The browser reads views assembled by the Worker from published programme data and enrichment records. Sources: [Film/Person read contract](../reference/domain/film-person.md), [enrichment API](../reference/api/enrichment.md), [prediction diagnostics](../reference/api/prediction-diagnostics.md), and [fingerprint/prediction ADR](../adr/0014-fingerprint-prediction-lifecycle.md).

The film detail page also ranks a short list of similar films from the programme films already loaded and from the catalog answer the Finds section cached. Watchlist films come first, up to four when the catalog also has a candidate, and catalog films fill the remaining slots to eight; a source with no candidate may use all eight. A displayed reason signature may appear at most twice across that slate. A catalog candidate is ranked only after its own prediction passes the Finds floor: a fresh rating of at least 4.0, a chance of 4+ of at least 0.70, and a confidence that is known and not low. A shared mechanic, credit or execution value does not waive that floor, and the list is not padded when fewer catalog films qualify. A watchlist film with a stored rating and a substantive overlap is not held to that floor. An unused generic execution label does not replace a shared mechanic, person or plot engine that is still under the signature cap. The film page itself is at most 1280px wide. Synopsis, credits, history, comparison and availability stay in a 760px column; the similar-films grid uses the full page width, so a desktop viewport shows more than one card across, and below 760px the grid is one column. Cards follow the Finds card: a poster already on the loaded film, or the Finds placeholder when it has none, with the corner rating and forecast when those numbers are already loaded and fresh. A watchlist card keeps the neutral surface and a catalog card uses the Finds green surface. That list adds no reader and no arrow: a generation mismatch drops the catalog half, and the page does not request the catalog route itself. `watched: true` on the enrichment view, or a stored actual rating, marks a substantive match «Пересмотр» and keeps that film out of a prediction-only suggestion. An unknown `watched` value stays a watchlist film. The page also drops a catalog candidate when that already-loaded view confirms the same film is watched or rated under its own id or a trusted alias. The catalog route remains the eligibility authority.

**Score badges on compact cards (AIR-517).** A Finds card and a Similar-films card share one renderer (`scoreBadgesHTML`). «МОЯ ОЦЕНКА» (`actualRating`, a half-star value 0.5 to 5.0, of the loaded `GET /api/enrichment/view` entry for the card's own id or for the other spelling of the same Film that the loaded data verifies; a conflicted or disagreeing mapping shows nothing) comes before «ПРОГНОЗ» (the stored, non-stale prediction), both with one decimal. They are independent facts and each badge renders only when its fact exists: one fact is one badge, two are two, none renders no `film-score-badges` container. There is no placeholder, no «—» and no space reserved for a missing badge (the layout classes `has-score` and `has-both-scores` follow the badges actually shown). Finds still exclude watched and rated films, so a Finds card normally has the forecast badge alone. No new request, identity guess or storage read is added; a stale prediction stays hidden.

**Availability is one computed lifecycle** ([ADR 0114](../adr/0114-film-availability-lifecycle.md), [reference](../reference/domain/film-availability.md)): per film, `available` when a show has ended inside the 72-hour catch-up, `soon` when there is none but a show is on air or starts in the next 24 hours (ready at the effective END of that show: the EPG end, else start plus the film runtime, else unknown and never invented), `unavailable` otherwise; an archive outranks live and future. It is a pure function of the shows already in hand (`src/domain/film-availability.js`), served to the browser as `/film-availability.js` and embedded in the Worker. Both catalog endpoints take `availability`, `availableNow`, `availableUntil` and every `shows[].status` (`future`, `on_air`, `archive`, `expired`, `unknown`) from it, so they cannot contradict each other; no storage, no extra read. Finds reads it with the film runtime and the browser clock (archive first; a «Скоро» film keeps a short line, the show and its channel, and the expected archive time lives only in the label of the hourglass), and one shared poster mark, `soonBadge`, is the only place an hourglass is drawn: list cards, Finds, similar films and the film page poster call it, so a film with a ready archive never shows it and it disappears with the clock. The main period selector «Все показы / Архив / Скоро» and the shortlist «Доступно / Скоро» read the same lifecycle per film (`filmLifecycle`): «Архив» is `available`, «Скоро» is `soon`, «Все показы» their union, so the counters, the cards and the shortlist are the same disjoint sets, fixed by the filters and not by the selected tab, computed over the shows of the selected channels when a channel filter is on, and changing with the clock without an EPG reload; old `period=future` and `shortlist=today` links and presets are normalised ([programme filters](../reference/domain/programme-filters.md)). The film page «Показы» block reads it too: `filmAvailability` in `public/app.js` shows the film state as one pill («Доступно», «Скоро», «Недоступно») and takes each row's phase from `FilmAvailability.showPhase` with the film runtime, over the shows the programme window still holds, so the pill never contradicts its own list. One show is one compact line (channel and start once, no counter, no «nearest» panel); several shows are grouped, archive first, then «В эфире» (on air, or started with an unknown end), then the repeats, three rows per group and the rest folded in a `details`; with a ready archive only the first repeat is visible. «Архив доступен до» is the latest ready start plus 72 h (never moved by a repeat), «Последний шанс» appears only for that ready archive inside 8 h, and for `soon` the block names the expected end of the show («архив появится после…», or «по длительности фильма» when the end came from the runtime, or «время появления архива неизвестно») with a note that it is a schedule-based calculation and no provider is probed. The poster hourglass stays the single `soonBadge` of AIR-435; the shared clock tick that removes it also re-renders this block, with no request. The ordinary programme card's own show groups follow the period model above.

The programme screen lists watchlist members from `films`. Catalog Films of the pinned run remain on the completed-read payload as `catalogFilms` for Finds, the film page and the prediction queue; they are not mixed into the main grid. Availability on those other surfaces comes from the completed-read showings, not from leftover `shows` bytes on a catalog record ([ADR 0142](../adr/0142-epg-owned-generation-addressed-discovery-slots.md) decision 13). The flowchart above is unchanged: the Worker still assembles one programme view; the completed read is that assembly.

Finds (`GET /api/catalog/available`) and the prediction view (`GET /api/enrichment/view`) keep serving while Discovery writes continuation or status: the catalog is a single-statement snapshot, not a generation-fenced walk ([ADR 0147](../adr/0147-discovery-user-read-snapshot-fence.md)). A 503 on those routes is «каталог / прогнозы сейчас недоступны», never an empty collection. A first failed load is not drawn as zero films. The flowchart above is unchanged: the Worker still assembles the same views.

The currently published programme generation determines which shows are available. A show is not a permanent Film property. The derived view index and prediction queue speed up views and work selection; neither owns Film identity or replaces stored fingerprints, predictions or actual ratings. The view index is derived from D1 on every read: the prediction, fingerprint, impact and mood fields come from `prediction_pointer` and `prediction_fingerprint`, and `watched`, `actualRating` and `watchlist` from `ratings_view`. `GET /api/enrichment/view` recomputes the mood from the derived fingerprint. A film with no row has none of those fields ([ADR 0078](../adr/0078-d1-authoritative-prediction-domain.md), [ADR 0192](../adr/0192-retire-view-index-selector-and-raw-object.md)). Prediction freshness depends on fingerprint and taste-profile identity, not on a broadcast slot. Reviewed localization takes precedence over provisional titles, subject to the active read path.

**Owner admin read path:** the `/admin` page is one more browser reader of the published programme generation, through the Worker route `GET /api/admin/*` and the same Access application as the rest of the site. The Worker reads the generation pointer, that generation's programme object and the two localization dictionaries, projects Film and Person records in memory with the pure domain views and pages them with the query library; every answer is pinned to the version tuple of those objects, and a changed dictionary or generation refuses the old pin instead of mixing snapshots. The catalogue reads no enrichment object, lease or ledger. Those catalogue reads write nothing. The five admin sections share a left column of the same width as the programme filters, listing Каталог, Здоровье данных, Архив Letterboxd, Лимб and Постеры. On a narrow screen that column stacks above the page. It does not replace the catalogue queues or the limbo filters. The film card and the person card each have a confirmed POST, listed below. The health view at `/admin/health`, the same document as `/admin`, adds one more read-only route that, beside the same pinned snapshot, reads the view index, the taste profile, the prediction queue, the raw resolver continuation record and the generation's catalog copy, each a single object, and evaluates them in the Worker with the pure data health model; sources it cannot read in a bounded way (the resolution ledger, the Letterboxd import receipts) are reported as unknown, never as healthy. The page leads with a status banner and three KPI cards (critical groups, warning groups, and sources that were not read), then a selectable inbox beside one panel labelled «Выбранная проблема», whose heading is the selected group's name. Routine waiting is a separate compact list, and on a narrow viewport that panel sits under the selected row of either list. Subsystem cards and sources sit in a closed disclosure. The lead counts groups that need a look, or says there is no urgent step when the groups are only routine waiting and unverified sources; a subsystem `attention` is not relabelled. A critical or warning group stays above routine waiting. Choosing a row only repaints the answer already on screen. Each issue names what happened, how many are affected and the kind of next step: waiting, a manual look, a link to a page that has its own confirmed workflow, a read-only list, a pipeline step with no page, or unknown evidence. A link only navigates, and the page says so once. An unsupplied source, and an unknown poster backlog, do not display an affected count of zero. The health answer has no film titles, so several films in one group are numbered links; stored ids, codes and thresholds stay in a closed disclosure. An empty issue list is not a success when a subsystem is unknown or the declared film coverage is partial. The limbo inventory route `GET /api/admin/limbo` reads the same pinned snapshot plus two of those single objects, the prediction queue and the view index, adds both versions to its pin and lists the films that are not progressing with the cause each waits on; it reads no per-film enrichment object and no body of the resolution map or the discovery ledger. It also lists unresolved discovery observations from the published generation's observation index: the manifest and the shards that hold records, with the decision map and the ledger compared by etag only. When that index is fresh or stale and holds records, the same request reads once the resolver continuation, the discovery status and the resolver lease — the objects one observation-recovery read already opens — and asks whether the published-EPG authority is known. Those reads do not write. An absent object is a fact and the gates run. An unreadable continuation, status, lease or published-EPG authority is not a completed check (`checked` false, action counts null) and does not fail the inventory. Mode `d1` is a known refusal of an otherwise eligible reconsider (`unknown_authority`). Their etag tokens join the pin only on that request. Without a readable index, or when the index holds no records, observations are reported as unavailable or empty and no coordination object is read. Each item carries `ownerAction`. A film is never an action on this page. An observation is an action now only when the existing reconsider gate or the existing identity offer is available; a stale index, an ordinary wait and an unknown gate are not. The limb page opens on that action-now queue. The verdict is above the filters. A stale list pin is reread once without the pin; the write confirmation is not. The same answer carries one signature per canonical film already in the inventory. The page offers a refresh when that displayed read is at least 60 minutes old. A successful read that finds 50 of those films differ says the list was updated and does not ask for another refresh. An etag change with the same signatures does not. A failed retry clears the selected detail and does not keep the previous action count. Returning to the tab paints the hour line and does not read. «Все состояния» keeps the four display groups — a decision is required, an ordinary step is not scheduled and no owner write is offered, the ordinary pipeline is expected to move them, or the state could not be checked — and a missing, stale or partial observation index is not presented as an empty limb. The owner expressly accepted the fourth display group on 2026-11-03, superseding the original three-group wireframe. Coverage above the queue is one line. The counts, the search field and the cause buttons sit in closed disclosures after the queue. Cause indicators are compact buttons: a pressed cause is included, an unpressed cause is excluded, and releasing every button lists nothing. The number on a button in the action queue is confirmed actions; the inventory count is labelled separately. «Обновить» rereads the snapshot and does not start a pipeline. «Найти запись» matches titles of rows already shown and does not query the inventory, because the route has no text-search parameter. Escape in that field clears only the local title. «Сбросить фильтры» clears it with the server filter and reads again only when a server filter was set. The queue is the working surface under the verdict. The selected record is the column beside it on a wide screen and the block under the row on a narrow one; the block names the record by title, year and kind, the wide column takes that title as its accessible name, and a jump on the selected row moves focus to the heading while arrow keys stay on the queue. The action sentence follows the control on the row and does not treat a data-health eligibility flag as a confirmed write. Filters, the snapshot and the pipeline thresholds sit in closed disclosures after that queue. The exact-key search for one verified match sits after those disclosures, closed. From an item on `/admin/limb` the page can open the prediction recovery read `GET /api/admin/prediction-recovery`, which for one film reads the objects the prediction diagnostics read (queue, profile, view index, generation programme and catalog, that film's fingerprint and prediction) plus the runner lease and the latest run, through a bucket wrapper that exposes only `get`, and explains the film's state with a pure assessment; it offers no action and there is no targeted prediction writer. The poster coverage route `GET /api/admin/posters` reads the generation pointer, the two catalog copies and the remembered-answer index `epg/catalog-poster-negatives.json`, counts them with the maintenance route's rule and shows the next bounded batch of the actionable queue. An absent index is empty; an unreadable one makes the coverage unknown. From an observation item the `/admin/limb` page opens `GET /api/admin/discovery-recovery`, which explains one observation from its index shard and the resolver's small coordination objects through a `get`/`head`-only wrapper. All six are projections of published R2 objects, not a canonical Film database, and none executes an action. Four admin pages can cause a write, each only after the owner confirms one exact scope:

- `/admin` sends two confirmed same-origin POSTs. Each card has **Добавить вариант** and **Сделать предпочтительным**. The film command writes one `films` entry of `admin/owner-reviews.json` and copies `people`. The person command writes the `people` entries of one command and copies `films`. «Добавить вариант» stores an alias and leaves the preferred value. «Сделать предпочтительным» stores a new preferred value ([film title review](../reference/domain/film-title-review.md), [owner name confirmation](film-person-lifecycle.md#owner-name-confirmation), [ADR 0072](../adr/0072-owner-reviewed-localization-overlay.md)). Search is first. Quality queues are a closed disclosure, and a zero count is omitted unless that queue is already selected. On a wide screen the results sit beside the selected card. At 800px and below the open card replaces the result list until «К результатам». A change of search or quality queue clears the selected record immediately, including on a narrow screen, so the result list is visible again. The confirmed sentence appears only after the card is reread and proves the command. The health view of the same page stays read-only.

- `/admin/posters` calls the existing owner-only maintenance route `POST /api/enrichment/catalog/posters` for one bounded run, or for one explicit recheck of a remembered answer. The page leads with four counts from the read-only coverage: without a poster, actionable now, held (a 90-day no-poster answer or an identity mismatch), and without a verified TMDB id. An unknown figure stays «неизвестно» and is not shown as zero. Batch size, call budget and start stay in a closed «Дополнительные настройки», with defaults of one call of ten records. «Перечитать» only repeats the GET. A failed repeat labels the previous figures as not current and does not offer a run. The run is still preview, exact confirmation, progress with «Остановить после текущего пакета», then a receipt that names each outcome. History is a later card, «История этого браузера», and is not a server audit. That route's lease, generation and compare-and-swap guards are the [poster hydration path](discovery-and-resolution.md); the same call records a terminal TMDB refusal in the remembered-answer index under those guards.
- `/admin/settings` ([admin API](../reference/api/admin.md#catalog-enrichment-control-apiadminsettings)) reads the effective Catalog Enrichment state once on open and shows it with the pause/resume checkbox of the existing C1 control. Its only write is one same-origin POST of `pause`, `resume` or `retry` to `/api/admin/settings/catalog-enrichment`, followed by a fresh read; it cannot activate the producer or widen a scope.
- `/admin/limb` sends the guarded `discovery_reconsider` action: two same-origin POSTs, a server-issued confirmation and then the execution, which may change one decision of one observation under the resolver lease ([discovery recovery](discovery-and-resolution.md)).
- `/admin/letterboxd` can send two further same-origin POSTs after a preview. The page walks five steps one at a time: the ZIP, freshness and completeness, the exact difference, one confirmed batch, then the receipt. Only the current step is mounted. Back and Continue do not send a request, and the receipt step appears only after a write attempt. The difference leads with additions, changes and skips. An incomplete comparison is not called a full check, and the archive hash, file hashes, programme run id, chunk id, plan hash and scope fingerprint stay in a closed disclosure. Confirm stores the issued confirmation only. A separate execute writes one chunk of at most 50 compared films through the baseline commit. A `partial` or `unknown` receipt is not success. A lost response is `unknown` and is not described as a write that never started. «Прервать ожидание» stays outside the file form while a confirm or commit is in flight. Cancelling that wait does not undo the Worker, and the same confirmation stays available for a manual retry. Another file or declared time marks that preview stale and disables write controls that have not already been sent; an unresolved command stays until the owner forgets it in the tab, which undoes nothing on the server. A lost confirm offers that same forget control. HTTP 409 `in_progress` stays replayable with the issued confirmation. `unknown` can be sent again only as that same confirmation, using the file from the check that issued it. There is no rollback control ([confirmed apply](letterboxd-ingestion.md#confirmed-archive-apply-proposed), [ADR 0068](../adr/0068-letterboxd-archive-confirmed-apply.md)). The preview request itself still writes nothing.

The Letterboxd archive page `/admin/letterboxd` sends the owner's export ZIP in one `POST /api/admin/letterboxd-archive/preview`. The Worker reads it in memory, compares it with single named R2 objects through a get-only wrapper and answers with a bounded proposal. That request stores nothing and applies nothing ([archive preview path](letterboxd-ingestion.md#owner-archive-preview-no-write), [ADR 0066](../adr/0066-ephemeral-letterboxd-archive-preview.md)). The archive itself is still stored nowhere. The confirm and execute clicks, when the owner makes them, each upload the ZIP again. A later check asks for the same file again, and it does not start while a confirmation or a write in this tab is still `unknown`.

**Cross-source identity work read ([ADR 0170](../adr/0170-unified-identity-work-inventory.md)).** `GET /api/admin/identity-work` is a second read-only route beside `GET /api/admin/limbo`, over three typed lanes (EPG observation from D1 Discovery, Letterboxd rated candidate from the per-archive R2 candidate document, Film from the existing evaluation). The programme page, the film screen and the expanded events screen share one status footer in the document flow. The same footer is mounted on the admin sections (`/admin`, `/admin/health`, `/admin/letterboxd`, `/admin/limb`, `/admin/identity`, `/admin/posters` and `/admin/settings`) and is filled only by `GET /api/enrichment/footer/status`. The block of `public/app.js` and `public/status-footer.js` that reads and paints it is kept byte-identical (a test compares the two copies, and the two copies of the markup), so the pages cannot drift apart. «Показать детали» stays the disclosure; «Админка» stays the link after it.

**Compact footer.** The footer answers four questions instead of listing thirty-three technical fields: what is in my collection, how useful are the recommendations, how fresh is the programme, and does the automation work. It shows five indicators — **Коллекция** (unique films), **Прогнозы** (current predictions over the local database), **Новые 4.0+** (new films, with the 4.5+ share), **EPG** (the last confirmed update) and **Автоматизация** (an aggregate of the seven integrations: the poster retry described below and, since AIR-593, the canonical Producer «Обогащение каталога») — and, behind «Показать детали», exactly four groups of twenty rows:

| Group | Rows | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Моя коллекция | всего фильмов, просмотрено, оценено, в watchlist | Counters overlap and are not a partition. One film is one verified canonical TMDB identity; an id that no stored record links to a TMDB id is its own film and is never merged with a catalog film. Watched means a confirmed watched fact, rated means a stored rating (the view index after the compiled ratings seed); «not on the watchlist» and «not in the Diary» are never taken as watched |

**New films.** A «new» film is a verified catalog movie outside the watchlist that the owner has neither watched nor rated, with a prediction that is current for the present profile and fingerprint and passes the same floor as «Находки» (rating 4.0 or more, chance of 4+ at least 70%, effective confidence not low). The two ranges do not overlap: 4.0 to 4.4 and 4.5 and above. The show window is not applied (it counts the collection, not the airing now), and a film with two catalog records counts once at its best current prediction. The exact formulas and the sources are in the [footer API reference](../reference/api/enrichment.md#compact-footer-read-model).

**Unknown is not zero.** A count that cannot be read is «нет данных», never 0 and never 100%; an empty collection is a known 0. When the server could not read a source (it names it in the optional `degraded` list, for example the Discovery ledger while its transfer is `loading`), everything that needs it arrives as `null` and is shown the same way; the footer itself keeps answering instead of failing as a whole. A failed read before any success says «статус недоступен» for the five indicators. After a successful read, a later failed refresh keeps the last confirmed counts on screen as a labelled stale snapshot («Не удалось обновить. Показаны данные от …») and shows the health indicators (EPG, automation and the integration rows) as «статус недоступен», so an old «всё работает» is never presented as current; a recovered read clears the note. The kind of the last failed read is kept on the note as `data-failure` (`http_<status>`, `timeout`, `network`, `bad_json`, `render`), invisible and removed by the next good read; a read that has not answered in 45 s is aborted, so one hung request cannot stop the 60-second polling (AIR-519).

**Removed from the user footer, kept in the API and in diagnostics:** the cold queue, cache outside the window (both duplicates), films waiting for a background prediction outside the window, retry deferrals, the current and next batch, the historical journal, channels, checked transmissions, films without metadata, titles awaiting review, the separate «Последний запуск» block and the separate «Модель вкуса» block. All of these are still fields of `GET /api/enrichment/footer/status`, `GET /api/enrichment/status` and the Admin Health diagnostics; the footer links to `/admin/health` and `/events` for them.

On an admin page the events link opens `/events`. On the programme page and the film screen the footer events link is a real `/events` link too: a plain click opens the in-page events screen (leaving the film screen first, so Back returns to the film), while a new-tab, copy-link or modified click uses native navigation to `/events`.

**Ratings event details (AIR-393).** The events screen and the compact feed draw the optional `details` of a `ratings` event as expandable groups of the films the run changed (new views and ratings, changed ratings, a removed film's rating found, a removal reference settled), in the same renderer as the watchlist event. Names and ratings come from the stored event, so opening the screen adds no read and no external request. A film links to `/film/tmdb_<id>` only when the event carries a TMDB id; an unnamed film reads «Название недоступно», never its id; the unchanged Diary rows are never listed, and a capped group states the remainder. An event without `details` keeps its short summary. Contract: [events](../reference/domain/events.md#ratings-run-result-ratings).

**Watchlist and viewing marks (AIR-513).** The same screen links each film named by a `watchlist`, `started` or `unstarted` event to its local page: `/film/tmdb_<n>` when the event carries a trusted numeric `tmdb` (resolved movie identity at write time), otherwise `/film/<id>` when that id is a film-route id and is not an unverified `tmdb_` prefix. The title and the year are never used as an identity. A watchlist event's displayed time is the run completion, not the scan. Opening the screen adds no request. A new `started` mark takes its film, title, year and trusted TMDB link from the canonical D1 Film (AIR-516), so a retained film outside the programme and the watchlist can be marked; the frozen `metadata.json` and `programme.json` are not read and the R2 mark and event objects keep their authority ([events reference](../reference/domain/events.md)).

**Returning to the programme.** The film screen, the events screen and every admin page show one link, «На главную», first in the content container under the header (`public/return-home.js`; the same label, style and place everywhere). The programme's own path, query and hash travel in the URL of each page opened from it as the single `return` parameter, encoded once: on the film and events URLs, on the footer events and admin links, on the admin section links and in the admin catalogue and limbo URLs. Nested navigation (film to events to film, between admin sections) and a reload keep it because it is in the URL, not in script state or storage. Only a canonical internal home target is accepted: pathname exactly `/`, no scheme, host, `//`, backslash or control character. A missing or rejected value means `/`, the canonical home, so a bookmark or a deep link never restores an old session and there is no open redirect. A screen opened straight from the programme page returns by Back (scroll kept, no extra history entry); in every other case the link is a plain navigation to the target. A film card link takes the programme's current state as its target before a new-tab open or a copy.

**One frame for the programme, the film page and the events screen.** These three routes share the programme's two-column workspace, with the same sidebar width, left offset and main column, so moving between them changes the content and not the layout (on narrow screens the sidebar stacks above the main column, as on the programme). On the film page the sidebar stays whole, with every filter and the compact event feed, and the film replaces only the programme column; a filter changed there (a person, a facet, a channel) is applied at once and is part of the return target in the film URL, so «На главную» returns to the set actually chosen. On the events screen the sidebar shows only «Тип событий» and the full feed is in the main column. The feed is one node that moves between the sidebar and the main column, so it is never rendered twice and there is one fetch. The type choice and the guarded automatic search it starts are described in the [events reference](../reference/domain/events.md#type-filter-on-the-events-screen).

**Default sort and Finds links (AIR-382, AIR-626).** The programme list opens sorted by «Для меня» (`personal`) when the URL has no `sort`, after «Сбросить» and with or without a search query; the select's default option is the same. The select offers exactly three sorts, «Для меня» (`personal`), «Название · А–Я» (`title`) and «Сначала исчезающие» (`expiring`), and `visibleFilms()` has exactly these three comparators. A chosen sort is kept and written to the URL, an explicit `personal` included, with or without a search query, so a reload, history entry or shared link keeps the choice (the implicit default writes nothing). The search box never changes the sort: typing, changing or clearing a query keeps the chosen or implicit sort, and the query only decides which films are listed (the `searchRank` membership check in `eligible`), never their order. The retired `time` (AIR-382) and `relevance` (AIR-626) sorts have no option and no code: an old `?sort=time` or `?sort=relevance` link, history entry, return target or saved preset, like any unknown value, falls back to the default, keeps the query and every other parameter, and the retired parameter is dropped at the next URL normalization; `normalizePresetQuery` drops it from a stored preset on every read and write, so no preset data migration is needed, and the preset mark (`presetKey`) treats such a preset as the same scenario as the default sort. New links and presets never write `relevance`. The title of a Finds card and of a catalog-only similar film is **not** a TMDB link: since AIR-387 it opens the internal film page `/film/tmdb_<n>` when the film's identity is verified (see [One permanent film URL](#one-permanent-film-url-air-387)) and is plain text otherwise. The Letterboxd icon is always the shared `letterboxdLink(film)`, in the Finds card title and in every similar-film card title (watchlist and catalog-only rows alike; the rows carry the film object unchanged, so `url` and `letterboxdUrl` reach the helper). The target comes from one pure resolver, `letterboxdTarget(film,{catalog})`, in this order: (1) a validated canonical Letterboxd film URL, which always wins: `letterboxdUrl`, and for a watchlist/local film also its `url` (for a catalog-only film `url` is its TMDB page and is ignored here); (2) only for a catalog-only film (the Finds card and catalog rows of the similar list) whose `canonicalIdentity` is exactly `tmdb:<n>` and agrees with a positive-integer `tmdbId`, Letterboxd's documented redirect `https://letterboxd.com/tmdb/<n>` (AIR-384), labelled «Открыть в Letterboxd по TMDB id»; (3) otherwise no icon. The redirect is a navigation aid, not a Letterboxd id, slug or provenance: it is derived at render time, never stored, never put into `letterboxdUrl` or an identity record, and never fetched or verified, so the page does not claim the film exists on Letterboxd. Watchlist/local rows never get the fallback; TV identities, mismatched or malformed ids and title/year-only matches get no icon; a slug is never built from a title ([catalog reference](../reference/api/catalog.md#letterboxdurl)). Clicking the icon opens a new tab (`rel="noopener noreferrer"`); a watchlist title in the similar list still opens its internal film page. No storage, authority or identity behaviour changes.

#### One permanent film URL (AIR-387)

`/film/tmdb_<n>` is the permanent URL of a TMDB movie, for a watchlist film and for a catalog-only film alike; `/film/<letterboxd id>` stays a working alias that opens the same page. The page is the existing film screen (sidebar, «На главную» with the return target, history, similar films); there is no second UI.

**Film error page (AIR-531).** When no film is proven (`missing`, `absent`, `retired`, `conflict`, `unavailable`, `error`, or while loading) the page shows only the title, status text and the way back: the comparison block, its result, similar films, shows and actions are hidden, and the comparison selection saved for that film id is cleared (not only hidden), so nothing returns when the id is valid again; a page that is only loading keeps its saved selection. Only a temporary failure (503, 5xx, network) is retried; absent and retired are settled. The HTML document itself carries the matching HTTP status ([catalog reference](../reference/api/catalog.md)).

- **Which watchlist films earn the permalink.** Only a film whose record proves a movie: `tmdbLink.status` is `resolved`, its link type is `movie` and its id equals `tmdbId` (positive integer, at most 12 digits). A TV link, an unresolved or contradictory link, a record written before the field existed, a number held by two loaded films, or a mismatch keeps the Letterboxd id, which always works. Nothing is guessed from a title or a year; no storage id, rating, fact or prediction key changes. Cards link to the permalink; opening an alias of a proven film rewrites the visible URL in place (`replaceState`, same entry, `return` kept), so Back, Forward, reload and deep links behave as before.
- **What a TMDB route resolves to, in this order:** a loaded programme film (by its own id, or by the permalink it proves); a catalog film the Finds already hold for the loaded generation; otherwise one permanent read, `GET /api/catalog/film/<tmdbId>` ([reference](../reference/api/catalog.md#permanent-catalog-film-api-catalog-film-tmdbid)). `GET /api/catalog/available` is not the lookup: it is limited to the hot window and to eligibility. Two loaded films proving one number show a conflict, never a guess. Catalog to watchlist keeps the same permalink; the page then shows the watchlist film.
- **States.** `loading` is shown until the programme could still resolve the alias (never «не найден» before both sources answered); `absent`, `retired`, `conflict`, `unavailable` and `error` each have their own message and no film is invented. A found film is cached for the session; absent, retired and conflict are settled; a transient failure is asked again after 15 seconds on a later render. A cached film on another generation than the loaded programme is refreshed once per generation in the background while the old one stays on screen; a failed refresh keeps it, and an answer pinned to another run is shown and not asked for again until the programme moves.
- **A catalog-only page** shows the shared blocks (title, original, year, runtime, genres, poster, validated Letterboxd link by the AIR-384 rule, the stored prediction and rationale, characteristics, people, availability, history, similar films, and the owner's Letterboxd rating and watched mark when they are known). Zero shows is the honest placeholder; an absent prediction is «Прогноз ещё не готов», a stale one is shown marked «ожидает пересчёта», and the rating card appears only for a film that has a known rating or a watched mark. It hides «Начал просмотр», the comparison block and the synopsis; a watchlist film keeps them. History reads the existing `GET /api/enrichment/history` with the film's own id (`tmdb_<n>` on a permalink); with the Film/Person marker `open` the server bridges it to the verified storage ids under which the stored prediction versions lie (D1 identity claims only, [contract](../reference/api/film-history.md)), so a retained Film's original and later versions show on its permanent page (AIR-475). At `open` the page itself is served for any retained canonical Film with a trusted TMDB claim, with or without Discovery, a rated admission, the programme or the watchlist, and it shows the owner's rating and watched mark, the stored forecast (judged by the compatibility evaluator: `compatible` shown with its profile version, `stale` marked with the evaluator's reason) and the stored fingerprint, all projected from the D1 domains that own them (prediction pointer and fingerprint, ratings view) for exactly the requested `tmdb_<n>` and the Letterboxd id the D1 identity bridge verified, whether or not the R2 view index holds those keys (the index is only the seed and the fallback of a domain that is not d1; never a title match). The history reads the facts and the Diary from the ratings domain and the forecasts from the prediction domain, not from R2 copies.
- **Similar films** rank from the catalog film's own prediction and fingerprint (`similarCatalogView`); the source film is excluded by its verified identity from both lists; no further request is made.
- **Boundaries.** Presentation and one read-only GET. No D1/R2 write, no authority, identity or admission change, no provider call. A retired or removed catalog record is reported as such; the page never claims to restore it.

**Important side-effect boundary:** do not assume all HTTP GET requests are pure reads. Some existing view/event handlers can initialize or recover persisted state; consult the [deployment runbook](../runbooks/deployment.md) and [GET side-effect tests](../../tests/get-side-effects.mjs) before operational probing.

**Authority:** the read cutovers are done per domain; which store each reader uses is in [authority per domain](persistence-and-migration.md#authority-per-domain). The published programme and watchlist are read from D1 at the pinned generation; the view index is derived from the D1 prediction and ratings rows on every read. There is no raw base, no selector and no raw writer, and `r2` is no rollback ([runbook](../runbooks/view-index-source.md)).

#### Poster retry in the footer and the Events Log

The footer integrations gain a sixth source, `posters`, and the events feed a type `catalog_posters` ([ADR 0179](../adr/0179-weekly-automatic-catalog-poster-retry.md)). Both read the same R2 checkpoint and the same projection as the Monitor, so the footer line, the Monitor status and the last weekly event agree. The footer stays a pure, public read with one extra object read; the admin pages mount the same footer. The source feeds the aggregate **Автоматизация** indicator and the details disclosure only; the five top-level indicators are unchanged. #### Canonical Producer in the footer (AIR-593)

The footer row «Обогащение каталога» (source `canonical_producer`, between «Распознавание» and «Прогнозы») reports the EPG to canonical Film/Person Producer from the Monitor's own bounded reader (three indexed D1 statements) and the Monitor's own verdict function, with the progress clock the Monitor stored in `monitor/state.json` (read in the same single read as the poster markers, never written). The footer and the Monitor therefore agree on the same facts. It shows the exact published generation, the last success while current, processed units of the current pass (units, not films), and a stop reason by recovery class in Russian. A failure is «есть сбой» in the collapsed **Автоматизация** indicator; held and unreadable are never green. ## Watchlist membership reader seam (ADR 0186, dormant, AIR-569)

`composeWatchlistRead` is the one seam in front of `GET /api/watchlist`, `GET /api/programme`, the Finds read (`/api/catalog/available`) and the permanent film page. With the Watchlist marker `epg`, absent or no D1 binding it returns the generation's `watchlist` document unchanged. With `d1` it returns the composition of the **current snapshot membership** and the pinned generation's stored records and shows (`WatchlistComposition.composeWatchlist`), reading the metadata cache only for ids with no stored record. It is read-only (ADR 0107): a read never writes, repairs or moves the programme generation. The marker is read fresh at every request (no cache), the snapshot read is fenced on the generation and on `writes open`, and the route re-reads the marker after its last async step: a switch or a hold in between is a retryable 409 (`watchlist_authority_moved`), never an answer in the old state. A held, closed, absent or malformed authority is a typed 503 and never degrades; only a storage error is `temporary`, and then ADR 0177 allows a **validated standalone Watchlist mirror** only (labelled `source: watchlist_authority`, `snapshotVersion`, `lastSuccessAt`), never a programme or an EPG copy. A new member's identity is a verified canonical one only: the Film/Person reader must be `d1`, the record must not be `identityBlocked`, and the reader's own fence must hold. The composed document is additive (`snapshot`, `removed`). Nothing in this section is active in production until the separate owner cutover.

The offline derivation is **delivered dormant** ([AIR-665](https://trello.com/c/EgOYnLRE): `src/film-readiness.js` and `src/film-readiness-composition.js`, not in the bundle). The D1 **reader switch is not done**: no read model, route or diagnostic reads it today, only the domain diagnostics compute the old booleans; the wiring belongs to the aggregate activation ([AIR-648](https://trello.com/c/WKSwJOwm), own owner GO). Readiness gates no route, producer or eligibility.
