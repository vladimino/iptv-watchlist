# Catalog poster hydration: scoped maintenance and authorization

Catalog-only films offered in the «Находки» section show the year placeholder when their catalog record has `poster: null` and the `metadata.json` fallback has nothing for them. The decisions are [ADR 0020](../adr/0020-owner-triggered-catalog-poster-hydration.md), [ADR 0048](../adr/0048-permanent-catalog-poster-maintenance-route.md) and [ADR 0063](../adr/0063-catalog-poster-negative-index.md); the route, outcomes and write rules are in the [catalog reference](../reference/api/catalog.md#poster-hydration). This page is the procedure. The route is a permanent owner-only maintenance tool for restoring posters, for example after a mass TMDB discovery failure; it has no switch of its own (the former `CATALOG_POSTER_HYDRATION` is retired and inert). Access is the Worker's owner-only Cloudflare Access. A bounded poster sweep is a **candidate for L2 remediation** under [authorization policy](../process/authorization-and-escalation.md), because the current route has generation, lease, budgets and receipts. AIR-358 does **not** authorize new autonomous sweeps: production writes still require a scoped owner-approved execution plan until an independent contract/runtime review explicitly enables an enforceable L2 standing authorization. A code deploy approves none. The route itself is a bounded manual operation; the separate [weekly automatic retry](#weekly-automatic-retry) is a different path with its own gate. The owner page `/admin/posters` runs the same steps through the same route, with its own preview, confirmation and receipt ([below](#through-the-admin-page)); the direct calls of the plan stay valid.

## Plan

1. **Read-only count.** `POST /api/enrichment/catalog/posters` with `{}` through the Access-protected site, from the same origin. Record `programmeRunId`, `catalogRecords`, `missingPoster`, `verifiedMissing`, `actionable`, `noPosterHeld`, `identityReview`, `unverifiedMissing` and the sampled `ids`. No TMDB request and no write happen. `ids` are the actionable queue, not every verified record without a poster. `unverifiedMissing` records are out of scope for this procedure. `noPosterHeld` waits until the date on the index; `identityReview` is not retried by this plan. A `503` `Poster negatives unreadable` means the queue is unknown: do not treat it as empty and do not continue.
2. **Bounded calls until `done`.** Under the execution approval, call `{"dryRun": false, "limit": 10}`, then repeat with `"cursor"` set to the returned `nextCursor` (an empty string means "start again at the first record"). Stop with success when a response has `done: true` (`nextCursor` is then `null`). `done` means the actionable queue was walked. Records TMDB has already refused are not in it, and `missingPoster` can stay above zero. Keep the per-film `outcomes` and `writes` of every call as the receipt. An outcome with `negativeSaved: false` was not stored; the next ordinary call from the start will ask for it again.
   - A `409` with `Resolver is writing the catalog`: wait for the discovery chunk to finish and repeat the same call; never override the lease.
   - `rate_limited` with `retryAt`: wait until `retryAt`, then repeat the same call.
   - `generationChanged: true` or `leaseLost: true`: repeat the same call later. Look at `writes`: a copy written before the stop already holds correct posters; the next call re-reads both copies and completes the other one.
   - To keep a sequence of calls on the generation recorded in step 1, add `"expectedProgrammeRunId": "<programmeRunId>"` to every call. Once another generation is published the route answers `409` `Programme generation changed` before any TMDB request and any write; start again from step 1 for the new generation.
   - **Attempt bound:** stop after at most 5 consecutive calls that hydrate nothing and return the same `nextCursor`, or after 30 calls in total. Report the `retryable` ids of the last response with their outcomes (`tmdb_failed` errors, `catalog_changed`, …) to the owner. Do not skip a retryable record by editing the cursor without a separate decision; `done` is only meaningful for a cursor chain that started without a cursor.
   - `no_poster_on_tmdb` is remembered for 90 days and then becomes actionable again if the poster is still missing. `tmdb_id_mismatch` is remembered until the verified id changes and is not retried by an ordinary call. It is stored only when TMDB returned a different valid numeric movie id. An HTTP 200 with a missing, null, zero or non-numeric id is `tmdb_failed` and stays in the ordinary queue. To ask one remembered id again before its date, under the same execution approval, send one call `{"dryRun": false, "recheck": ["<tmdbId>"], "expectedProgrammeRunId": "<programmeRunId>"}` with no cursor. Do not edit the sweep cursor to reach it.
3. **Read-only verification.** `GET /api/catalog/available`: `posterCoverage.catalog` grows by the hydrated films that are currently available and `missing` shrinks; for the films the owner reported, `films[].poster` is a URL. A repeated step 1 shows the new `actionable` and the remembered counts. Then the owner's visual smoke on desktop and mobile: at least some «Находки» cards show an image instead of the year. A film whose TMDB answer was `no_poster_on_tmdb` keeps the placeholder; that is not a failed run.

## D1 authority (AIR-515)

While Discovery is `d1`/`open` the steps above run against the `discovery_admission` rows: counts, the queue and the preview come from D1, one D1 batch writes the poster, and nothing in R2 that holds the catalog is read or written (only `epg/catalog-poster-negatives.json`). `writes` has the single entry `discovery_admission`; `generationChanged`/`leaseLost` mean the batch was not written and the same call can be repeated. A `503` with `storageUnavailable` is a D1 or authority fault (nothing was written, no R2 fallback); with `writeUncertain: true` the D1 batch failed and may have committed, so re-read coverage on `/admin/posters` before any repeat. The runner stops with `storage_unavailable` or `write_uncertain` (no automatic retry). The merge of this change authorizes no sweep and no copy of historical R2-only posters: that needs its own operation-specific owner GO, after the read-only inventory of R2 posters that D1 lacks.

## Through the admin page

`/admin/posters` ([reference](../reference/api/admin.md#poster-coverage)) is the same plan with the bounds built in. It is behind the same Cloudflare Access; it never configures the TMDB token, never touches the discovery lease itself and has no flag. Each production run must fall under a pre-approved bounded plan or a future separately enabled L2 standing contract; UI confirmation and technical bounds remain enforced.

1. **Read-only count.** Opening the page or «Перечитать» is one `GET /api/admin/posters`: the four lead counts of step 1 (without a poster, actionable now, held, without a verified id), the actionable records, the remembered answers and the ones without a verified TMDB id, and the first batch the next call would process. Nothing is written and TMDB is not called. A warning appears when 20 or more verified records have no poster; it does not mean all of them can be filled. When the generation pointer, either catalog copy or the negative index is missing or unreadable the page says «Покрытие неизвестно: данные неполные», names what is missing, shows «неизвестно» instead of a number and offers no run; the page does not create or repair those objects, and restoring them is a separate approved operation, not part of this procedure. An absent index is empty, not a problem.
2. **Bounded calls.** The defaults are one call of ten records from the saved position. Batch size (at most 10), call budget (at most 30) and the start (the position the last run in this browser saved for this generation, or the start of the queue) are under the closed «Дополнительные настройки». Tick the confirmation of that exact scope and press «Запустить». The page re-reads the queue first and refuses, with nothing sent, when the generation or the first batch changed. It then sends the calls of step 2 one after another, each bound to the confirmed generation with `expectedProgrammeRunId` (a generation published meanwhile is refused by the route before TMDB and before any write), and stops by itself on `done`, the budget, five idle calls, `retryAt` (the next preview waits until then), a generation change, a busy or lost resolver lease, `TMDB is not configured`, a rejected request, or any answer it cannot read. «Остановить после текущего пакета» stops between calls. A held record is not cleared by this ordinary run.
3. **Receipt and verification.** Each film name in the preview, the receipt and the remembered-answer list opens its TMDB page in a new tab; records without a verified TMDB id have no link. The receipt lists the stop reason, the totals, every record's latest outcome and, under «Технические подробности», every call with its writes per copy; a partial write between the two copies is named. An outcome that says the verdict was not stored was not written to the index. The page re-reads the coverage and shows before → after. Keep the receipt text as the operation record, then do the read-only verification of step 3 above.
4. **One remembered record.** The page lists at most ten remembered answers in each group, in id order, with no paging or search. The counts still include every remembered record. «Перепроверить» is offered only for an id in that sample; an id outside it is a direct call of step 2, not a control on this page. «Перепроверить» on one row, then its own checkbox and «Перепроверить выбранное», sends the single recheck call of step 2. It is not the sweep and it does not move the saved position. «Перечитать» on the coverage only re-reads.

After an unknown result (a network failure or an unreadable answer) the page keeps the previous saved position and asks for a re-read: the call may or may not have written, and a repeated call is harmless (`already_present`). The page's run history is kept only in that browser and does not decide which records the next run requests.

## Failure recovery

The two catalog copies are separate R2 objects and are not written atomically together. A call that stops between them leaves the published generation's copy with the poster and the live catalog without it; the response reports it per copy (`writes`) and per record (`writtenTo`). Nothing has to be undone: the poster is the TMDB asset of the record's verified id. The next call re-reads both copies, writes the missing one or reports the record again. A call that stops before the first write wrote nothing.

## Rollback

To stop, make no further execution call; nothing runs by itself. Posters already written stay: each is the TMDB asset of the record's verified id, in the same form the discovery writer stores, and nothing else in the record changed. Removing them would be a separate approved write that sets `poster` back to `null` for the listed ids from the receipts; no deletion step is part of this procedure. The route never writes the generation pointer, identity, shows or decisions. A code revert leaves `epg/catalog-poster-negatives.json` in place. Older code ignores it and shows those records in the ordinary queue again; deleting the object is not part of this procedure.

A code revert to a release that reads the former switch needs `CATALOG_POSTER_HYDRATION=on` restored on Worker `iptv` (owner authorization) before any execution call there; without it that code answers `409 Catalog poster hydration is off`. The dry run works either way.

## When to use

When the read-only count shows actionable records that matter to the owner, typically after a mass TMDB discovery failure. Each run needs an approved bounded execution scope (not necessarily repeated approval of every within-scope call), then the owner's visual smoke with at least some posters visible. Films TMDB has no asset for keep the placeholder until the remembered answer expires or a recheck finds one. A mismatch waits for an identity change or an explicit recheck. The manual procedure above is never scheduled; the recurring path is the weekly automatic retry below, which does not use this route.

## Weekly automatic retry

The decision is [ADR 0179](../adr/0179-weekly-automatic-catalog-poster-retry.md); the contracts are in the [catalog reference](../reference/api/catalog.md#automatic-weekly-retry), the [monitor reference](../reference/api/monitor.md#catalog-poster-stream) and the [events reference](../reference/domain/events.md#catalog-poster-weekly-report-catalog_posters). The code, the Monitor stream and the cron entry `24 3 * * *` UTC in `wrangler.jsonc` exist and are tested offline. **Nothing is activated or verified in production by the documentation or by a merge:** the cron takes effect only on a deploy that carries it, and activation needs a separate explicit owner GO on the Cloudflare schedule. Positive hydration is unproven until a real `poster_path` appears on a natural weekly run.

**What runs.** The first firing of each week (weeks start Sunday 00:00 UTC, normally the Sunday firing) runs a sweep of the verified Films without a poster whose hold has expired or that have no index entry, through the same core as the owner route. Other days the firing only resumes a run that is `pending` with its retry time reached. A firing with nothing to do reads one R2 object and writes nothing.

**Read the status (read-only).** Use only reads that cannot write:

1. `GET /api/monitor/status`, stream `catalog_posters` (`state`, `detail`, `freshnessAt`, `nextExpectedAt`).
2. The `catalogPosters` section of `GET /api/admin/health`, and the footer source «Постеры» (`GET /api/enrichment/footer/status`).
3. The weekly report in the Events Log, type `catalog_posters` (`deferred` and `final`, with checked, hydrated and still-without-poster counts).
4. `GET /api/admin/posters` for the coverage counts. It is side-effect free.

**What each state means.**

| State | Monitor status | Meaning and what to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `healthy_idle` | healthy | The week has its receipt. Zero due films and 90-day holds are normal. Nothing to do |
| `grace` | healthy | No checkpoint yet and the first firing is not overdue. Nothing to do |
| `waiting` | waiting | A run is pending with a scheduled retry, or in progress. Wait for the next daily firing |
| `overdue` | delayed, then degraded | The week's sweep (or the first one) has not left a receipt 26 h after its due firing. Check that the cron entry is in the live schedule (read-only schedules readout) and that the deploy carried it |
| `stalled` | degraded | An undelivered event or a stuck run or retry. The next firing replays owed events without TMDB; if it persists, report it to the owner |
| `lost` | degraded | A checkpoint existed and is now absent. Report it; do not recreate it |
| `unknown` | degraded | The checkpoint is unreadable or invalid, or the Monitor is off. Report it; never treat it as healthy |
| `failed` | degraded, critical | The run failed, the retry cap of six was reached or the rate limit persisted over two due firings. Owner decision needed |

**Do not.** Run the cron by hand, trigger a firing, call TMDB or POST the owner route to "make the week green"; edit, delete or recreate `epg/catalog-poster-weekly.json` or the Monitor markers in `monitor/state.json`; reset a lease; or create a Scheduled Task for it. A deploy does not run the sweep and grants no permission for a data operation. Activating the cron needs the owner's explicit GO on the Cloudflare schedule, with a read-only readback of the live schedule afterwards.

**Rollback.** A pull request that removes `24 3 * * *` from `wrangler.jsonc` and its dispatch in `src/worker.js` (and the branch), with its own deploy and a readback that the live schedule no longer lists it. Stored posters, negative-index entries, the checkpoint and delivered events are not rolled back; the manual route and its procedure above are unaffected.
