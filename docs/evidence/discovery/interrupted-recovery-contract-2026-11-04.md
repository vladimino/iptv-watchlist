# Interrupted discovery recovery: local contract

**Captured:** 2026-11-04, local `node tests/interrupted-recovery.mjs` on an in-memory bucket. No production R2, D1, Worker, MCP call, flag or Scheduled Task
**Input:** synthetic continuation, status, diagnostics ring, lease and authority objects. The production series was not re-read. The earlier read-only snapshot on the card (stage `complete`, continuation `paused` / `interrupted` / `settled`, `lastBatch.checkpoint=cheap_pass`, no `nextCatchUpAt`) is the shape the fixture copies, not a new inventory
**Method:** the pure gate over those objects, then the Worker resolve and hourly step routes against the fake bucket. Refusals are checked by comparing the continuation bytes and by listing every write outside the continuation-diagnostics ring
**Scope:** contract test only. Not a live recovery and not a measurement of why a production invocation ended
**Verdict:** **SYNTHETIC CONTRACT HOLDS.** An hourly step on `interrupted` writes no continuation and answers `stop`. `chunks: 0` returns `eligible` for a same-generation `cheap_pass` status and writes nothing. One confirm runs one chunk, drops the interruption count, and a second confirm does not run another. The forced resolve still runs and still sets `force`. Stage `epg`, a different generation, a live or malformed lease, a future backoff, a finished same-hop status, a missing status, a claimed checkpoint, a zero budget, a missing TMDB token, a held, loading or unreadable authority, and the workflow executor all refuse with no continuation write and no fetch. A prior hop's finished status is eligible and does not rewrite a stored verified decision. An expired lease is eligible. A compare-and-swap loss answers `busy` and leaves the series interrupted. A confirm hop killed at `cheap_pass` fail-closes on the next ordinary step. A due `chunk_budget` record is not spent by the confirm, and the ordinary step still opens the next series
**Used by:** [AIR-341](https://trello.com/c/r8tfPFjt), [ADR 0087](../../adr/0087-guarded-interrupted-discovery-recovery.md) (Proposed)

## What is not established

The platform cause of the production hops is unknown. `cause: unknown` on a closed diagnostics entry is that limit, not a finding that the Worker ran out of memory or that an earlier EPG change caused the stop. `cheap_pass` shows the cheap pass was stored and the final continuation checkpoint was not. This note does not confirm either fact against the live bucket.
