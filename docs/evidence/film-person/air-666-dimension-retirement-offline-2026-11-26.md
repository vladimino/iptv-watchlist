# AIR-666: guarded retirement of legacy `film_dimension` copies, offline evidence

Captured 26 November 2026, revised 27 November 2026 after independent review; offline: synthetic SQLite fixtures built from every migration and a read of the source. **No production read or write, no Cloudflare access, no retirement run.** The code is dormant; this report proves the offline contract only and says nothing about live rows.

## Consumer inventory (source read, not live proof)

At this commit the Worker's SQL selects only `identity` (`src/catalog-d1.js`) and `localization` rows of `film_dimension` (`src/catalog-d1.js`, `src/canonical-catalog-reader.js`, `src/film-aggregate.js`); the fingerprint and prediction dimensions of the admin health view are derived from the view index and the profile. No `src` module other than the dormant importer, parity reader, reconciler and the canonical writers names the three retirable dimensions. The holdpoint of the operation ([AIR-648](https://trello.com/c/zh0cfDWK) reader switch and no remaining D1 consumer) is proved from live evidence before any GO, not by this read.

## Cost (fixture measurement, not a threshold)

30 Films with plain copies of the three dimensions: a dry run of all 30, 61 D1 queries and at least 652 rows read, no write; applying 1 Film, 41 counted queries (at most 13 per Film), 2 batches and 23 changed rows (CAS and receipt rows included); applying 29 Films, 433 counted queries in 58 batches. The provider limit is 1,000 queries per Worker invocation; the pass counts its own queries and stops before the next Film does not fit.

## Not proven

Behaviour on production D1 (limits, latency, row counts), the live cohort, the real coverage supplier and the guards it will assert, the checkpoint storage (here a file stand-in) and any consumer outside the repository. Each belongs to the separate operation and its owner GO.
