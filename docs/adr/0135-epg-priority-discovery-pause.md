# ADR 0135: Discovery admission pauses for the daily EPG publication and stays paused until that publication is handed off

**Status:** Accepted
**Original decision date:** 2026-11-13
**Recorded at:** 2026-11-13
**Approval source:** owner decision on [AIR-458](https://trello.com/c/RrtTkNg1) (13 November 2026): EPG is the upstream supplier of candidates and has priority over downstream Discovery; Discovery waits for a successful EPG sync with no business TTL, including past two hours; step leases stay bounded; an in-flight chunk finishes or its lease expires, and no new chunk is admitted. This record is the mechanism for that decision. It is Accepted, not implemented and not activated, and it authorizes no production operation.
**Related work:** [AIR-458](https://trello.com/c/RrtTkNg1); incident context [AIR-420](https://trello.com/c/J0dit94i) and [AIR-444](https://trello.com/c/lSSrrJdE); [ADR 0089](0089-shared-orchestration-reliability-contract.md); [ADR 0093](0093-discovery-workflow-cron-reconcile.md); [ADR 0098](0098-discovery-workflow-step-failure-recovery.md); [ADR 0100](0100-epg-cloudflare-workflow-executor.md); [ADR 0103](0103-projector-exclusive-window.md)
**Supersedes:** None. It qualifies, and does not rewrite, one sentence of [ADR 0100](0100-epg-cloudflare-workflow-executor.md) decision 1 and the matching sentence of [ADR 0089](0089-shared-orchestration-reliability-contract.md): EPG still does not run Discovery, and it may soft-busy-wait for a live Discovery lease. It adds a refusal in front of the reconcile of [ADR 0093](0093-discovery-workflow-cron-reconcile.md) and the successor create of [ADR 0098](0098-discovery-workflow-step-failure-recovery.md). It does not change the 300 s deadline of [ADR 0103](0103-projector-exclusive-window.md).
**Superseded by:** None

## Context

On 13 November 2026 the natural EPG run published at 17:44:34 Europe/Madrid, 44 minutes after the 17:00 due time (the publication target is 15 minutes). About 22 minutes of that was cooldown after three EPG failures, two classified `resolver_busy` and one `unknown`, while Discovery chunks held the resolver lease. EPG also waited about 4.5 minutes on a `projector_window`. The memory failures in `seal_ledger` / `seal_publish` are a separate incident and are not decided here.

Those busy collisions are counted today. `publishD1Generation` and the other publication paths that cannot take `discovery_lease` throw `Resolver is writing the catalog`. The continuation diagnostic maps that text to `resolver_busy`, and `EpgOrchestration.classifyThrown` then maps an unrecognised throw to `step_failed`, a transient failure. The shared budget then sleeps (60 s, then 5 min, 15 min, 1 h) and can quarantine the run. A Discovery chunk is not a fault in the publication.

Discovery is downstream of the published generation. It has no candidates until EPG publishes, and a new chunk during the publication only competes for the same lease. The owner decided that Discovery waits. The wait is not the projector window: [ADR 0103](0103-projector-exclusive-window.md) is a mutual exclusion of at most 300 s with its own expiry, and this record does not move the projector or give EPG priority over it.

The pure decision this record specifies lives in `src/domain/epg-priority-pause.js` and is not imported by the Worker. With the table verified absent, admission stays as it is today. An unreadable answer about whether the table exists fails closed.

## Decision

1. **Priority.** EPG publication is ahead of Discovery on the shared `discovery_lease`. The order covers the daily run, a rollover, cron `reconcileDiscoveryWorkflow`, a Workflow successor, and every manual or admin path that would take a new Discovery lease (a chunk, `discovery_reconsider`, an identity correction, poster maintenance, the interrupted-confirm path). An in-flight Discovery chunk is not killed. Its holder may `renewLease` until that chunk ends; a new `acquireLease` is refused. EPG's own `acquireLease` (`role: 'epg'`, including `sealStep`) is exempt, so the publication can take the lease once the holder drops it. A caller that omits `role` is Discovery, so a forgotten call site cannot slip through the pause. The projector window is unchanged and independent: while it is `requested` or `held`, Discovery is also refused for `projector_window`, and an EPG step that meets the window still soft-busies until that window's own deadline ([ADR 0103](0103-projector-exclusive-window.md) decision 5). The window's deadline never lifts this pause, and this pause never extends the window.

2. **When admission closes.** Five minutes before the EPG due instant, 16:55 Europe/Madrid while the due time stays 17:00. The lead is `EPG_PRIORITY_LEAD_MINUTES` before `EPG_SCHEDULE`, not a second hardcoded clock. Five minutes is enough for the overlap to be exceptional: a paged chunk's candidate budget is 40 s (`PAGED_CHUNK_BUDGET_MS`) and the Discovery lease TTL is 90 s (`LEASE_TTL_MS`), and 300 s covers both. It does not promise an empty lease at 17:00. A chunk that is still renewing at the due time is the soft-busy case in decision 5, not a reason to interrupt it or to close admission earlier. The Worker cron is `0 * * * *`. Madrid's offset is a whole hour in both CET and CEST, so a tick exists at 17:00 Madrid on every date; the pause does not add a cron. The arming instant and the due instant exist on both DST transition days, because the transition is at 02:00/03:00. Conversion uses the IANA zone (`madridDayKey`, `madridInstant`), never a fixed offset.

3. **How long it lasts, and which column names the published day.** Until the owed Madrid date is published and the Discovery pin already names that same run. The published day is not derived from `published_epg_generation.published_at` (that column stores the programme report's `updatedAt`, which can fall on the next Madrid date) and not from the live EPG orchestration control `runKey` (the next `ensure` replaces it with the new date while the pointer still names the previous run). `programme_run_id` is an opaque id and carries no date. The future migration adds `madrid_run_key` (`YYYY-MM-DD`) on the one `published_epg_authority` row. `commitPublish` writes it in the same `UPDATE` that sets `programme_run_id` (`stageCommit`'s pointer batch). The value is the sync run's own frozen `runKey`, not the adopting Workflow's. `beginSync` sets `sync.json.runKey` once, to `madridDayKey` of that run's `startedAt`, when it creates the run. Later saves of the same `runId` do not change it. `beginSync` returns an unfinished `stage==='seal'` with that same object (ADR 0092), and `drive` treats every seal as usable with no day check, so after `day_rolled` the next day's Workflow can continue yesterday's seal. That Workflow's payload `runKey` is then today while `sync.json.runId` is still yesterday's run. `publicationDay` stamps the sync run's frozen `runKey` even when the adopting Workflow's key is a later date (`adopted_seal`). The pointer publish is not refused, and today's date is not written. `certifyWorkflow` is false in that case. The required later change, not made in this delivery, is that `act` must not set `doneAt` or `publishedRunId` on the adopting control, and the Workflow must continue so the next action starts a run for its own day. `drive` already starts one when a sync that is already complete belongs to another day; the gap is only the action that itself finishes the old seal. Live `src/epg-orchestration.js` is not modified here. That fence changes EPG completion even when the pause table is absent, so it is outside [AIR-459](https://trello.com/c/8AryOCug)'s promise that a missing table leaves today's behavior unchanged. No card and no AIR number exist for that fence. Creating one is a separate follow-up after this ADR's final acceptance, and it is not part of [AIR-459](https://trello.com/c/8AryOCug). A seal with no frozen key is `evidence_unreadable` for the stamp; the pointer commit does not invent a day from the Workflow or from `published_at`, and that completion also does not certify the adopting control. Quarantining a cross-day seal is not decided: that would change the ADR 0092 resume. This record lets the seal finish under its original day and keeps the new day's control open. The lease predicate, the arm statement, the pointer clear and the replay clear all read that column in the same statement or batch. A null column, the state of every generation published before the column exists, matches no owed day, so the pause fails closed instead of guessing. The migration does not backfill it from `published_at`. A `madrid_run_key` that is present and is not the owed date does not count as that date's publication, even when the pointer and the pin name the same run. There is no `expires_at` and no other automatic lift. The two facts move in different transactions, and this record does not merge them. `sealArchive`'s committing batch (and the older `beginLedgerSeal.finish` batch) sets `discovery_authority.published_programme_run_id` to the new run while `published_epg_authority.programme_run_id` still names the previous generation. That lag does not lift the pause and does not admit the new run. The row is cleared only inside the existing pointer batch of `PublishedEpg.publishGeneration` (`sealPublishStep`'s `pointer` unit, `stageCommit`), and only when that same batch sees `discovery_authority.published_programme_run_id` already equal to the `programmeRunId` being published. The batch does not write the pin. If the pin does not yet equal that run, the pointer publication stays the publication it is today and the pause row stays `paused`. A later batch clears it once both name that run: the replay branch of `publishGeneration` when the pointer already names the run, or the next Discovery acquire's batch. Neither admits while they differ. A repeated pointer publication is the replay branch, not a second publish. A failed, quarantined or stalled EPG run does not release the pause. A missing Workflow instance does not release it and does not create one; the existing EPG `ensure` remains the creator, and the existing `late` (15 min) and `stalled` (2 h) read model, plus the end-of-day alert, remain the escalation. The pause record names the owed date, so a pause that crosses midnight is the same pause, not a new silent one. Before the next day's arming instant the owed date is still yesterday while yesterday is unconfirmed. At the next 16:55 the owed date becomes that new day even if yesterday never published; a late publication of the older day after that instant does not lift the pause (the same rule as the reliability contract's "a missed date is not backfilled once the next date is due"). An owner override is a separate CAS that sets `override_day_key` to the owed date. It admits Discovery for that date only for the `programmeRunId` the published pointer already names. A different requested id is `stale_generation`. A missing or non-string requested id stays closed (`evidence_unreadable`). An override value that is not a `YYYY-MM-DD` date makes the row unreadable and does not admit. The override does not publish, and the next arming instant drops it. Removing it closes admission again.

4. **The gate is a predicate inside the write, plus a row arm in the same batch.** Proposed table `discovery_epg_priority`, one seeded row (`id` 1, `state` `clear` or `paused`, `owed_day_key`, `override_day_key`), no expiry column. The migration file is part of the implementation card and is not applied here. `tablePresent: false` means the implementation verified that the table is absent, and only then does admission stay as today. `undefined`, `null` or any other unreadable answer is `evidence_unreadable` and admits nothing. While the table is present and the row is missing, or its `state` is not `clear` or `paused`, or a paused row's `owed_day_key` is not a `YYYY-MM-DD` date, or `override_day_key` is present and is not such a date, Discovery admission fails closed and writes nothing. There is no cron at 16:55. The hourly cron does not arm the row. The first caller does, lazily. Admission does not wait for that arm: the lease `UPDATE` for a Discovery `acquireLease` carries the predicate in its own `WHERE`, using the bound `now`, the bound arming instant, the bound owed `YYYY-MM-DD`, `published_epg_authority.madrid_run_key` and `programme_run_id`, the Discovery pin, and the override column. The owed date is computed in the Worker with `madridDayKey` and passed in; it is not read back from `published_at` or from the orchestration control. A row that is still `clear` therefore cannot admit during the window. A refused lease update changes no lease row, so it cannot itself write the pause row. The same `batch()` appends a second statement that sets the row `paused` for the owed date only where that same predicate is still true. If the lease update matched, the predicate was false and the second statement matches nothing. The caller's receipt is the read-back of the lease id it just tried to take, not a check before the batch. `renewLease` does not carry the predicate. `writeContinuation`, `writeWorkflow` and `importSegment` carry the same predicate on the statement that moves the generation, so a lease-free writer cannot bump it during the pause; their batch may arm the row with the same second statement. `ensureDiscoveryWorkflow`, `reconcileDiscoveryWorkflow`, rollover and `armStepFailureRecovery` do not create an instance while the pure decision refuses, and the workflow-state write carries the predicate so a create that raced still cannot persist a new owner. A restart or a lost Workflow reads the row, the pointer and the pin again; none of them lives in one isolate.

5. **Soft-busy.** An expected collision is `nextAction: wait` with a closed `reason`: `epg_priority` (this pause; `nextCatchUpAt` is a 60 s poll, `DISCOVERY_EPG_PRIORITY_POLL_MS`, and the poll does not lift the pause), `projector_window` (the window deadline, unchanged), `lease_held` or `lost_race` (the live lease expiry). When the pause and the projector window both apply, the reported reason is `epg_priority`, so a caller cannot treat the window deadline as the end of the pause. None of these increments `consecutive` or `total`, sets `cooldownUntil`, or is classified `step_failed`, `unknown` or HTTP 502. Discovery health while the reason is `epg_priority` is `waiting`, not `stalled`. EPG health is unchanged: a publication that has not happened is still `late` and then `stalled`. A D1 failure stays `d1_unavailable` (transient, counted, no fallback store). `authority_held`, `authority_loading`, `evidence_unreadable`, `evidence_contradictory`, `stale_generation` and `generation_mismatch` stay fail-closed. The diagnostic fields are the reason, the owed date, the lease owner kind (`discovery`, `projector`, `epg`, `none`) and the hold expiry. No Film id, SQL, continuation body or other payload.

6. **After publication.** The old Discovery series does not take a lease and does not keep writing. A requested `programmeRunId` other than the one `published_epg_authority` already names is `stale_generation` (fail-closed, no retry loop), including while an override would otherwise admit and including while the pin has already moved ahead of the pointer. The intent that may run is that published id, through the existing generation fence and continuation, and only once the pin equals it. An interrupted older series stays fenced; recovery ([ADR 0088](0088-guarded-interrupted-discovery-recovery.md), [ADR 0098](0098-discovery-workflow-step-failure-recovery.md)) re-checks the pin and, during the pause, creates nothing.

7. **Crash and race matrix.** The implementation card's tests have to cover this table. `budget` `wait` means no failure count; `fail_closed` means the existing unsafe or transient class.

   | Situation | Result |
   | --- | --- |
   | Acquire before 16:55, yesterday published and pinned | admit |
   | Acquire at or after 16:55, today unconfirmed, row still `clear` | the lease `UPDATE` matches nothing; the same batch's second statement sets `paused`; `epg_priority`, `wait` |
   | In-flight chunk renews | renewal stands; no new lease |
   | CAS loses the lease to the holder | `lease_held` or `lost_race`, `wait`; EPG sleeps to the expiry and does not count it |
   | Lease expired, pause still open | the next acquire is refused; the pause is not released |
   | Worker or Workflow dies | the row and the pointer are still there; the next admission reads them |
   | EPG retry, rollover, quarantine, stall, or no instance created | pause stays; no TTL; EPG `late` / `stalled` / quarantine stay the escalation |
   | `sealArchive` has set the pin, the published pointer still names the previous run | pause stays; the new id is not admitted; the previous id waits as `epg_priority`, it is not yet `stale_generation` |
   | Pointer batch of `publishGeneration`, pin already equal to that run, and the bound `runKey` is the owed date | that `UPDATE` sets `programme_run_id` and `madrid_run_key` together and clears the row; it does not write the pin. A crash after the pointer without the clear leaves the row `paused` until the replay branch or the next acquire sees the pointer, the pin and `madrid_run_key` agree, and clears it. Only then may the new id acquire; the previous id is `stale_generation` |
   | Pointer and pin name one run, but `madrid_run_key` is null or a different date | pause stays; that match is not publication of the owed day |
   | The run's frozen `sync.json.runKey` is yesterday and the pointer batch commits after Madrid midnight | `madrid_run_key` stays yesterday, so yesterday's pause lifts; today's pause does not open until 16:55 today |
   | Next day's Workflow adopts a `stage==='seal'` run and its payload `runKey` differs | the pointer is published under the frozen sync day (`adopted_seal`); today's key is not written; the adopting control is not marked done and continues to its own run; today's pause stays. A seal with no frozen key is `evidence_unreadable` for the stamp and does not certify the control |
   | Midnight, then the next 16:55, owed day still unconfirmed | pause stays; the owed date rolls at the next arming instant; an override for the previous date does not apply |
   | Projector window during the pause | Discovery waits as `epg_priority`; EPG soft-busies for the hold it actually met; neither hold lifts the other |
   | Owner override for the owed date | admission opens only for the programme id the pointer already names; a different id is `stale_generation`; a missing id stays `evidence_unreadable`; clearing the override closes admission; nothing is published |
   | D1 error, held or `loading` authority | fail-closed, as today; the pause does not swallow it |
   | Reconcile, rollover, successor or manual/admin acquire during the pause | no new instance and no new lease |

**Not decided here.** The migration number and the applied schema, any flag, the production arming of the row, and the natural-run acceptance. Nothing in this record is activated. The memory failures of the 13 November seal are out of scope.

## Alternatives considered

- **A fixed TTL, including the projector window's 300 s.** Rejected by the owner. A publication can run longer than any bound chosen in advance, and an expiry would resume Discovery under a live EPG run.
- **Close admission only from the 16:00 cron, with no predicate on the lease statement.** Rejected. The cron does not fire at 16:55, and a check in one round trip followed by a write in another admits a chunk that raced the publication.
- **Derived only from the clock and the pointer, with no row.** Rejected as the whole mechanism. The statement does use that predicate so a late arm still fails closed, but an owner override and a visible owed date need a durable row. A missing row on a present table fails closed rather than pretending the pause is clear.
- **Interrupt the in-flight chunk at 16:55.** Rejected by the owner. The chunk finishes or the existing 90 s lease expires under the fence it already has.
- **Give EPG priority over the projector in this record.** Rejected as scope. The 13 November run also waited on `projector_window`. That window keeps its own contract; changing its schedule is a separate decision.
- **Count `resolver_busy` as a cheaper transient.** Rejected. The collision is not a failure, so it does not enter the cooldown budget at any price.
- **Quarantine a seal that is still open after Madrid midnight.** Not decided. `beginSync` and `drive` already resume that seal under ADR 0092. Stopping it would be a change to that resume, not a requirement of the pause. This record only forbids publishing it under the adopting Workflow's date.

## Consequences

Discovery does not start new work from 16:55 Madrid until the owed publication is handed off, which on a slow day is the whole publication and on a failed day is until an owner override or a later owed day's publication. That is the priority the owner asked for. EPG stops spending its failure budget on a live Discovery lease. The projector can still delay EPG by up to its own 300 s. A bug that omits the predicate on one generation writer would admit a chunk during the pause; the implementation card has to name every `acquireLease` and every lease-free generation writer. A bug that treats a refused lease update as the writer of the pause row would leave the row `clear` while admission is already closed, which is allowed, or would arm it when the predicate is false, which the second statement's own `WHERE` forbids. A bug that treats the 60 s poll as an expiry would lift the pause early; the row has no expiry column and the tests refuse one.

## Recovery and migration boundary

Reversible before activation: do not apply the migration. After a later, separately approved activation, code rollback does not delete the row or publish a generation. Clearing a stuck pause is the owner override, not a lease reset and not a TTL. This ADR authorizes no migration apply, flag, cron, Task, Workflow create, Discovery or EPG run, lease change or D1/R2 write.
