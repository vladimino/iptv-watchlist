## Exact calls (after owner GO only)

Access owner session. Preview is GET (read-only). Execute is POST. One film per request. New UUID `idempotencyKey` per film; do not reuse across films.

1. `GET /api/admin/discovery-recovery/policy-exclude?filmId=<film_id>` — require `available`, policy `concert` or `documentary`, related keys matching the table, admission `current`. Stop that id on `not_eligible`, `budget_exceeded`, `linked_admission`, or `unknown_authority`.
2. `POST /api/admin/discovery-recovery/policy-exclude` with header `X-IPTV-Admin-Action: catalog_policy_exclude`, JSON `{filmId, idempotencyKey}`.
3. Durable success: admission `superseded`, those observation keys rejected, current-generation `basis='discovery'` shows of those keys gone, film absent from pending/hot/cold/retries. `409 queue_unreconciled` means Discovery may have written and the queue did not settle — do not retry a different film until that id is diagnosed. `already_applied` retries the queue only.
4. Do not rewrite the published EPG generation. Do not delete ratings, fingerprints, prediction bodies, or Film/Person R2. Do not start Discovery, EPG, predictions, TMDB, Tasks, or flags.
