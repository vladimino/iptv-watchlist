**Results:** `tests/epg-producer-m2b-reader.mjs` passes (about 4 s); `tests/epg-producer-m2b-reader-mutation.mjs` kills 84 mutants (about 22 s locally), with the controls: a fresh work directory per check, the unmutated reader passes twice in one process, a neutral change survives, and a kill counts only as an assertion failure. The tests prove:

- the reader is a pure function over an injected `request` of the shape of `cloudflare.request`: no import, network, timer or environment inside its text, and its text runs as the body of an `execute` call and gives the same file as the function;
- ONE statement per request, in the printed order, one request in flight at a time; every statement sent passes `assertReadOnly`; parameters are `[after, pageRows]` or none;
- the assembled file passes `verifyResults` and `loadCopy` and equals the file of the independent reference reader; the loaded copy is row for row identical to the original (page sizes 2 and 100; a table of exactly one page; empty tables);
- every stop rule fires on the offending response and sends NO further request (the calls are counted against the tampered call): request failure, non-success envelope, wrong shape, missing or invalid metric, per-statement and running-total caps (rows read and milliseconds), schema version other than 21, unreadable marker (also on a request failure and on a non-success), invalid or over-cap count, page over size, a result row with other columns, a non-scalar value, a rowid that does not increase, a table that grew or came back short or with another maximum rowid, and a defect of the reader;
- a plan the reader does not recognise (another digest, a raised or missing cap, a statement that is not the planned SELECT, an unsafe name, a table twice) is refused before any request.
- the plan allowlist: a self-consistent, identifier-safe plan with an unapproved table (e.g. `schema_contract`) or an extra, missing, reordered or other column, tables in another order, a table left out, or a well-formed digest of another plan is refused with `plan_not_approved` and ZERO requests; the text of the reader contains exactly `COPY_PLAN` and the digest of `emit-sql`;
- the operator bundle is refused (`bundleText` throws, the bundle command exits 3 with nothing on stdout).

**Update after the Controller review of PR #600 (offline):** (1) the approved ordered tables, columns and statements digest are part of `String(readCopy)`, so the reader digest changed from `f711600e…d4bb` to `1cdbc0e55daf75435cbe39e99af89ef2bc099f652e91a5907cfe11e1b8530612`; (2) the single-result delivery of about 210,000 rows through one `execute` result cannot work (the connector returns about 6,000 tokens by default), so full-table capture through `execute` is BLOCKED and the runbook section 2b lists the delivery paths for the owner to decide, choosing none; no read budget was relaxed and no production probe was made.
