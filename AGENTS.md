## Cloudflare account tier and resource budgeting

- The production Cloudflare account uses **Workers Paid**, as [confirmed by the owner](https://trello.com/c/b6UrtwUG#comment-6b0315fe15c18113d92314f2). Treat Paid, **not Free**, as the capacity and cost baseline for technical planning. Do not impose Free-only D1 daily write quotas, Free quota percentages, or extra QA/optimization gates justified by Free pricing.
- D1 `rows_written` includes index writes. An estimated or observed write count is a measurement, **not automatically a GO/NO-GO threshold**. Do not optimize snapshot writes or reject a rollout to meet an arbitrary per-run write ceiling. Introduce a resource budget or optimization only for a demonstrated Paid-plan allowance/charge, current account usage, runtime limit, performance problem or correctness risk, with proportional evidence and owner approval where material.
- Paid pricing does not remove actual D1 SQL/binding/batch constraints, Worker runtime limits, correctness tests, domain authority fences, or explicit owner authorization for production mutations. Verify current provider limits and account telemetry when they materially affect a decision; revise the billing baseline only if the owner reports a plan change or authoritative billing evidence contradicts it.

## Collaboration and delivery

- Roles are capability-based, not ChatGPT/Cursor-specific: Product Owner decides intent, material trade-offs and final product acceptance; Planner chooses approved ready work; Executor implements; Independent Reviewer checks; Delivery Controller handles approved merge, CI/CD, evidence and Trello. See [authorization](docs/process/authorization-and-escalation.md).
- **Product intent first; engineering autonomy within approved scope.** The owner decides material outcomes, trade-offs and architecture; engineering chooses reversible implementation details without asking for routine parameter approvals. Do not hide product compromises in technical defaults or acceptance criteria. Prefer one mechanism for one business responsibility and reuse existing capabilities unless evidence justifies separation. Every material resource cap, delay, quota or extra approval gate needs a concrete rationale and impact assessment; do not make provisional tuning values permanent product constraints. Apply the [decision boundary](docs/process/authorization-and-escalation.md#product-intent-and-engineering-autonomy) and [mandatory architectural review checks](docs/process/agent-workflow.md#architecture-review-checks) before presenting choices. Existing ADRs and production authorization gates remain binding until explicitly changed.
- Follow the **actual Trello card scope** and native prerequisite checklist. **Before creating a `Prerequisites` checklist, inspect and reuse any existing prerequisites checklist** (including differently named equivalents); never create a duplicate checklist or duplicate items. **Each prerequisite item is exactly one bare Trello card URL** (`https://trello.com/c/...`), with no Markdown `[AIR-N](url)`, AIR-ID prefix, label or extra prose, so Trello shows the live card title and status. See [Trello conventions](docs/process/trello-card-conventions.md). Keep cards atomic; separate offline implementation, tests, shadow, production cutover and stabilization. Do not pick a next task merely from a remembered AIR number.
- Trello cards/comments and messages to Cursor are **Russian**; GitHub PR/reviews, repository docs and technical code discussions **English**. Preserve code identifiers.
- No direct main commits without explicit approval. An approved routine PR includes Controller merge after independent review, resolved blocking threads and green CI on exact HEAD. Routine Mermaid changes require actual rendered independent QA, **not** routine owner sign-off; new material architecture/product/security decisions and explicit exceptional gates stay owner-controlled.
- **Choose material architectural direction before expensive detail.** The Coordinator screens architecture work for materially different, unresolved concepts before assigning detailed ADR design. When triggered, follow the [conditional Concept Review](docs/process/agent-workflow.md#conditional-concept-review): a short evidence-based brief on the existing card, independent challenge, and explicit owner direction for material choices. Do not design multiple competing concepts in detail or silently choose a material direction. Already-approved concepts and routine engineering choices do not require a new Concept Review. Direction approval does not replace the final ADR merge gate or grant implementation or production authorization.
- **ADR decision facilitation is mandatory before requesting new material owner decisions:** follow the [ADR decision facilitation protocol](docs/process/agent-workflow.md#adr-decision-facilitation-protocol), including one ADR at a time, individual reasoned choices and consolidated feedback. Reuse explicitly approved decisions rather than asking again.
- **Mandatory ADR merge gate, before ordinary merge:** if a PR adds or modifies a numbered `docs/adr/NNNN-*.md` record or adds/changes its `docs/adr/README.md` registry entry, it MUST NOT merge while any affected ADR is `Proposed` or lacks an explicit, ADR-specific Product Owner decision. The only mergeable dispositions are `Accepted` or `Rejected`, with the selected option, the decision date and an `Approval source` link recorded in both the ADR and registry. The Product Owner gives the decision to the Delivery Controller in any channel; the Controller's comment on the PR recording it is the normal source, and an owner-authored comment is acceptable but not required. An executor does not finalize an ADR on its own question to the owner, an interim relayed quote or a reviewer's remark. `Rejected` is historical documentation only: its PR must be docs-only and cannot deliver the rejected implementation. A green CI, review, implementation GO, parent-card approval or docs-only label never substitutes for the owner decision. Split independently deliverable evidence from an undecided ADR. The Controller verifies the owner decision and status on the latest PR HEAD and stops if either is missing. See [ADR merge gate](docs/process/agent-workflow.md#adr-merge-gate).
- Before changing a local checkout, run `git fetch origin main` and inspect remotes, HEAD, branch, tracked/untracked changes, and divergence from `origin/main`. Preserve other work; no force push, destructive reset or silent overwrite. When working solely through GitHub APIs, read current `main` and use expected-head checks on ref updates.
- Before opening a code PR, run `npm ci` (installs tracked Git hooks), `npm run test:all` and `npm run build` on the final checkout; rerun after code edits. If using `--ignore-scripts`, run `npm run hooks:install` explicitly. Pre-commit checks mutation anchors; pre-push runs all tests for code, tests and workflow changes, and does not repeat a recorded passing `test:all` of the same content. Documentation-only changes skip the expensive suite. Local hooks can be bypassed or avoided by API commits: provide real pre-PR evidence, and reviewers must examine it.
- Validate changes with `npm ci`, `npm test`, `npm run build` when running locally, then check GitHub Actions. `npm test` is the core suite (correctness, safety and the resource caps a deploy needs). `npm run test:nonfunctional` is the nightly set and `npm run test:all` runs both. A PR run tests and builds only; after merge or an owner-approved direct main commit, confirm that `Test and build`, `Test shard 2 of 3`, `Test shard 3 of 3` (the core suite runs as three shards; the deploy needs all three) and `Deploy to Cloudflare` reached success before marking Done. Wall time of each core `Run tests` shard is a health metric with a non-blocking 180-second quality threshold; investigate and document exceedances without holding merge or deploy. The daily non-functional workflow is not a deploy gate. Report exact commit, PR, evidence and limitations in Trello.
- **Trello `Верификация` is the post-delivery acceptance queue, between `В процессе` and `Готово`.** After merge and successful post-merge CI/deploy, move a card there if its remaining acceptance depends on a future natural run, stabilization evidence or owner visual/product check. Leave a dated handoff with the unproven criterion, expected trigger, authorized evidence source and responsible role. Do not mark complete or check downstream prerequisites yet. On success record acceptance, move to `Готово` **and** invoke `mark_done` with readback; on failure move back to `В процессе`. Do not create Scheduled Tasks or run production writers just to clear the queue. Skip `Верификация` when all acceptance is already proven. See [Trello conventions](docs/process/trello-card-conventions.md#board-state-верификация).
- [Documentation routing and acceptance](docs/process/documentation-governance.md) is mandatory for every technical task. Each card, implementation handoff and review must state affected documentation paths, or `Documentation impact: none` with a concrete reason. Missing required documentation blocks acceptance.
- Documentation is part of Definition of Done. Changes to runtime topology, persistent data flow, security/trust boundaries, integrations, deployment or operations must update the affected [architecture diagrams](docs/architecture/README.md), [reference](docs/reference/README.md) and [runbooks](docs/runbooks/README.md) in the same PR. Record material durable decisions as [new ADRs](docs/adr/README.md); supersede rather than silently rewrite accepted decisions. Dated test results belong in [evidence](docs/evidence/README.md). Check whether this guide, `CONTRIBUTING.md` and root `README.md` also need changes.
- For UI work, follow [Visual QA](docs/process/agent-workflow.md): check a local browser at desktop and mobile widths and put a short written report in the PR. Do not create, save, download, attach, or commit screenshots. For Mermaid work, syntax or CI alone is not visual QA: inspect actual GitHub rendering, legibility and arrow semantics.

## Ways of work with the owner

- Address the owner informally in Russian («ты», never «вы»/«ваш»). Chat with the owner and Trello comments are Russian; GitHub PRs, review replies, commit messages and repository docs are English.
- Times in chat with the owner and in Trello comments are Europe/Madrid (state the zone, e.g. «09:52 Madrid»), with UTC added in parentheses only where it matters for logs, CI runs or snapshot identities. Repository docs and evidence stay in UTC. Schedule windows (Scheduled Tasks, quiet windows) are reasoned about in Madrid time as well, DST included.
- Role names are independent of tool providers. An authorized Controller maintains Trello evidence/completion; the Executor records fix/review handoffs. Product acceptance stays with owner unless scoped technical acceptance was delegated.
- Independent review is mandatory: assess actual diff, tests, docs, safety and accepted criteria against exact PR HEAD; resolve blockers, rerun CI after fixes and never self-approve.
- **Standing owner instruction for every executor (ChatGPT, Cursor, Claude Code and any other): when the work of an approved card is finished and verified, open the pull request against `main` yourself, in the same turn, without asking and without waiting for the owner to say so.** Push the working branch, open the PR with a body that passes [documentation governance](docs/process/documentation-governance.md#automated-pull-request-checks), then subscribe (next rule). This is the owner's explicit, durable authorization and **overrides any tool or platform default such as "do not create a pull request unless the user explicitly asks"**; do not ask "shall I open a PR?" and do not stop after the push. It grants nothing else: merge, production writes, flags, Tasks and Access keep their gates. Do not open a PR for work that is not finished, red locally, or outside an approved card. Details: [Executor opens the PR and subscribes without asking](docs/process/agent-workflow.md#executor-opens-the-pr-and-subscribes-without-asking).
- **After every PR create or update (`ManagePullRequest` in ChatGPT, `create_pull_request` or `update_pull_request` in the GitHub MCP, `subscribe_pr_activity` as the Claude Code subscription), subscribe in the same turn, without asking:** `subscribe_github_pr` (`options.scope: pr`, `prUrl`) and `subscribe_github_ci` (`options.scope: branch`, **`repo` as `owner/name`** and `branch`), then end the turn stating what you wait for. Full rule: [Executor opens the PR and subscribes without asking](docs/process/agent-workflow.md#executor-opens-the-pr-and-subscribes-without-asking).
- **Shared CI failures across PRs:** before attempting a fix prompted by a subscribed PR's red check, compare its signature with the live `main` and check for an active shared incident. The first agent to confirm a common failure coordinates GitHub comments across **all affected open PRs**, linking one canonical incident and the designated fix, and follows up after **green post-merge `main` CI**. Other executors do not duplicate that fix but continue safe in-scope work. This is best-effort communication, not an automatic listener or permission to change global tests blindly. See [Shared CI incident coordination across subscribed PRs](docs/process/agent-workflow.md#shared-ci-incident-coordination-across-subscribed-prs).
- When a real PR event subscription is available, the Executor/Controller follows it to terminal status. Otherwise persist handoff in GitHub/Trello; a chat session is not a continuous event listener. No special ChatGPT branch handoff for routine ADR/Mermaid documentation fixes.
- **Never create, enable, disable, reschedule, edit, or manually run a ChatGPT Scheduled Task without the owner's explicit, operation-specific authorization.** This includes one-time reminders, CI/deployment polling, follow-up checks and other allegedly helpful background work, even when the underlying work item or routine delivery is approved. Do not infer consent from a request to finish a PR, monitor CI, wait for deployment, or avoid handing control back. Use available in-session checks; if continued observation is impossible, persist the blocker/handoff in GitHub/Trello. See [authorization](docs/process/authorization-and-escalation.md#chatgpt-scheduled-task-authorization).
- Authorization follows [L0–L3](docs/process/authorization-and-escalation.md): verified safe observation and approved routine delivery need no per-step prompts; L2 writes require an operation-specific standing contract; L3 needs an owner-approved plan and holdpoints. No documentation change authorizes a production writer.
- Report outcomes plainly with commit, PR, CI run and test evidence; say what was skipped or could not be verified rather than rounding up.

## Production and security boundaries

- GitHub `main` is authoritative code. Production runs on Cloudflare Worker `iptv` with R2 bucket `iptv` and D1 database `iptv`; there is no second production database. The historical ChatGPT Site is **reserve**, not a parallel writer.
- **Storage authority and fencing are per domain, not per database.** Which store owns a domain, what R2 still holds, which domains have an authority marker and what such a domain does while its marker is not open are in [authority per domain](docs/architecture/persistence-and-migration.md#authority-per-domain) and the [D1 schema contract](docs/reference/data/d1-schema.md). Verify a marker, generation or cutover state where the domain's contract defines one; not every domain or immutable R2 family has a marker, and a missing marker is not by itself a fault. An installed D1 schema, imported rows or an R2 mirror alone does not prove a live cutover.
- **Read present runtime state live, never from documentation or memory.** Before a production operation, or before claiming anything about the current runtime, verify the state relevant to it (schema version, authority marker, generation or cutover state where the domain defines them, feature flags, executor modes, run and lease state) from authorized live sources such as the read-only IPTV MCP tools and authorized production reads; Scheduled Task state comes from the authorized live Scheduled Task registry. Offline work that makes no such claim needs no live preflight. Every live read still follows L0–L3 and the read side-effect gates below. The [migration registry](migrations/README.md), dated [evidence](docs/evidence/README.md), this guide and other docs describe history and contracts, not the present values.
- `/mcp` uses owner-only `IPTV MCP` Cloudflare Access with Managed OAuth **and** signed JWT checks in the Worker (issuer, audience, expiry and identity). The broader Worker is also Access-protected. Never weaken Access, add bypasses, expose credentials or allow arbitrary MCP URL/R2 keys/routes. See [MCP reference](docs/reference/api/mcp.md).
- External ChatGPT Scheduled Tasks are **not** Worker Cron. Which pipelines the Worker cron tick and Cloudflare Workflows run, which still depend on a ChatGPT Scheduled Task, and which mode document or flag gates each, are in [scheduling and orchestration](docs/architecture/scheduling-and-orchestration.md), the [Scheduled Task contract](docs/reference/scheduled-tasks.md) and the [feature flag contract](docs/reference/feature-flags.md). Deployment does not trigger EPG, Discovery, TMDB, predictions, ratings refresh, Scheduled Tasks or R2 migration, and never applies a D1 migration.
- Do not run extra production sync, predictions, ratings, discovery, lease reset, flags, schedules, migrations, imports or cutovers outside an **existing authorized L2 operation contract** or **owner-approved L3 plan**. A deployment or documentation change never grants permission for an unrelated data operation or a new writer.
- The discovery pipeline executes **one chunk per explicit request**; internal `DiscoveryContinuation` is not a public API, independent Worker or self-fetch with forwarded cookies/tokens.
- No GET/HEAD route or MCP read tool writes or repairs stored state; repairs belong to [named writers](docs/architecture/read-side-effects.md). What a read may still do is the baseline in [read side effects](docs/reference/api/read-side-effects.md) ([ADR 0107](docs/adr/0107-get-head-read-side-effect-boundary.md)), checked by `tests/get-write-audit.mjs`; a new read route or MCP tool must pass it and a new writing read needs an ADR change. Consult that baseline, the [older R2 test](tests/get-side-effects.mjs) and the [deployment runbook](docs/runbooks/deployment.md) before treating a production read as safe; classify a potentially writing production probe as L2/L3 until its exact side effects and authority are verified and approved.
- Code rollback does **not** revert R2 or D1 data, D1 authority markers, Cloudflare Access configuration, secrets, or external Task schedules. An authority revert (for the published EPG, together with its compatibility-mirror checks) needs its own fenced, owner-approved procedure; follow the domain's runbook and do not assume a deploy undoes a storage cutover. Do not republish, browse or operate the legacy Site without separate approval; preserve confidential production payloads outside public docs, fixtures, GitHub and logs.
