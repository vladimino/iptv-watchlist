# ADR 0118: An hour whose Discovery report cannot be completed is reported by a compact notice, never by unknowns, and delivered by an independent bounded path

**Status:** Accepted (Product Owner decision of 11 November 2026 on PR #523, review 5859945613: the emergency-delivery limit and the `lost` outcome are accepted; the normal Events Log has no one-year lifetime)  
**Original decision date:** 2026-11-11  
**Recorded at:** 2026-11-11  
**Approval source:** the Product Owner's scope of [AIR-439](https://trello.com/c/8bt41F8s) and the owner's product decision on [PR #523](https://github.com/vladimino/iptv-watchlist/pull/523) of 11 November 2026: every real scheduled hourly cron must produce a user-visible Discovery event with a truthful status, including when the report close fails; a missing event must not mean either a Discovery failure or a report failure; no three unknown values, no invented zeros; accumulated deltas and exactly-once attribution preserved; a failed compare-and-swap cannot persist its own failure, so an independent bounded idempotent fallback path with one stable identity per generation and hour is required, with eventual durable recovery when the journal is unavailable; accepted by the Product Owner on [PR #523](https://github.com/vladimino/iptv-watchlist/pull/523#pullrequestreview-5859945613) on 11 November 2026: the capacity of 8,760 pending notices and the exceptional `lost` outcome (the ledger full, or the journal and the ledger both unavailable) are accepted as the emergency-delivery limit; this imposes no lifetime on the normal Events Log  
**Related work:** [AIR-439](https://trello.com/c/8bt41F8s), [AIR-440](https://trello.com/c/cZJHgZeI) (one-time cleanup of the stored incomplete events, a separate owner-gated operation with its own inventory), [ADR 0111](0111-discovery-hourly-reports.md), [RCA of 11 Nov 2026](../evidence/discovery/air-439-incomplete-hourly-reports-rca-2026-11-11.md), [events reference](../reference/domain/events.md)  
**Supersedes:** [ADR 0111](0111-discovery-hourly-reports.md), the part of Decision 4 that writes an `incomplete` event with every number `null` when a close cannot be persisted, and the consequence "an incomplete event occupies its hour key". Everything else in ADR 0111 stands  
**Superseded by:** None

## Context

ADR 0111 gives every real scheduled hourly cron one report. When the close could not be persisted (a held projector window, lost compare-and-swaps, an unreadable record, an outbox at its limit, no record) it wrote an `incomplete` event whose counters and remainder were all `null`. The Events Log then showed «Отчёт за час неполный» with three «неизвестно», which says nothing, repeats, and crowds the «Находки» filter. On 11 Nov 2026 four such events were stored. Two things were wrong with them: the three unknowns (content-free), and the same fallback treating an unreadable **queue** as an unreadable **period**. The first revision of this ADR removed the event altogether; the owner rejected that, because an hour without a line cannot be told from a Discovery failure.

## Decision

1. **Every scheduled hour has a line with a truthful meaning.** A close that is persisted writes the normal report (real counters, queue status). An hour whose close cannot be persisted writes one **notice**: kind `report_failed`, «Отчёт за час не удалось сформировать. Если за этот период что-то было найдено, оно войдёт в следующий успешный отчёт.» It carries only `id`, `at`, `generation`, a typed `reason` (`no_record`, `state_unreadable`, `outbox_full`, `projector_window`, `write_conflict`) and `status`. It has **no counter, no queue fact, no `from`**: there is nothing to show as a number, an unknown or a zero.
2. **Discovery is named only when confirmed.** `status` is `failed`, `waiting` or `blocked` only when the reconcile of that firing answered so, and the card then adds the fixed sentence of that state; `running`, `idle` and every other value are stored as `null` and the card says nothing about Discovery. A report failure never implies a Discovery failure.
3. **Unread queue beside real deltas.** A persisted close whose queue reading is unavailable keeps its real counters, with `remaining` `null`, `queueEmpty` `false`, `incomplete` `false` and `queueUnreadable` `true`; the card shows the numbers and «не удалось прочитать» for the remainder.
4. **One hour, one event identity.** The notice and the report of an hour share the **same canonical identity** `discovery-hour-<12 characters of the generation>-<UTC hour>`, so the journal can never hold two events for one hourly firing. The precedence is deterministic: a **notice is put create-only and never replaces anything**; a **report replaces the notice of its hour** by a compare-and-swap on the notice's etag (and keeps an existing report as it is). A duplicate or concurrent firing that saves the close after a notice was delivered therefore leaves exactly one visible event, the report; a notice that arrives after the report finds its hour taken and changes nothing; a failed compare-and-swap leaves the report in the outbox for a retry, so the deltas of a report are never dropped because a notice got there first.
5. **The notice does not travel through the record that failed, and recovery is lossless.** A failed compare-and-swap, a held window or an unreadable record cannot persist their own failure, so the notice is put **create-only straight into the journal**. If the journal refuses it, it waits in a separate **ledger**, one small R2 object `events/discovery-report-faults.json` with its own etag and compare-and-swap. The ledger **never evicts**: it holds one compact tuple per undelivered hour (generation, UTC hour, typed reason, Discovery status; about 40 bytes, from which the notice is rebuilt exactly) up to an explicit bound of **8,760 hours (one year of hourly notices, well under 1 MB)**. Every later scheduled firing drains the ledger **before** it closes its own hour, **oldest first and at most 64 entries a firing**, so one invocation's R2 work stays bounded however long the outage was (a 200-hour outage is delivered over four firings); an entry leaves only after a confirmed put. At the bound the **newest** notice is refused and logged as `lost`; no older entry is dropped to make room. **Explicit trade-off for the owner:** a journal outage longer than a year of hours, or a total outage of the one storage bucket, leaves the hour with only its log line (`notice`: `delivered`, `queued` or `lost`), and the next report reaches back through its `from`; delivery during an outage is eventual, never claimed as instant. No D1 write and no operator action.
6. **Exactly-once attribution is unchanged.** A failed close never touches the accumulator (`report`, `seen`, `lastClosedHour`) and never moves a chunk into an outbox, so the next successful report carries the deltas once; a prolonged failure produces one notice per hour and the accumulator keeps growing. Empty stays strict: numeric zeros, no error and a `complete` continuation of the same generation.
7. **Nothing else changes.** One report per scheduled firing, deltas by commit time, the bounded outbox and its idempotent delivery, UTC hour identity, generation handoff, DST, no lease, no Discovery step, no TMDB call. Events stored before this decision, including `incomplete` ones, stay readable with their card; removing the four existing ones is [AIR-440](https://trello.com/c/cZJHgZeI).

## Alternatives considered

- **No event for a failed close (the first revision).** Rejected by the owner: a missing line is ambiguous between Discovery and reporting failures.
- **Keep the all-null `incomplete` event.** Rejected: three unknowns say nothing and look like a Discovery fault.
- **Store the notice in the executor record's outbox.** Rejected: it is the write that failed.
- **Evict the oldest ledger entries and count them.** Rejected in review: it would lose actual hourly notices.
- **A separate identity for the notice (`-report-failed`).** Rejected in review: a later successful close of the same hour would publish a second event.
- **A column or table in D1 for the ledger.** Adds a second authority surface and a migration; the journal and the ledger live in the same R2 bucket the Events Log already uses, with the create-only and compare-and-swap primitives the Worker already relies on.

## Consequences

- A bad hour has one compact, honest line; the Events Log never shows unknowns, zeros or a fake Discovery failure.
- One more small R2 object exists, written only when the journal refuses a notice, holding at most 8,760 compact entries (under 1 MB), never evicting.
- During a journal outage the notices are delayed, not instant; during a total storage outage the hour has only a log line.
- Rollback of the code does not remove stored events, the ledger or the `queueUnreadable` field; old code ignores them (a notice of an unknown kind is drawn by the generic Discovery card of the older code, and a report already replaced a notice).

## Amendment of 11 November 2026 (acceptance fail at 15:00 UTC): the close waits the projector window; a proven-empty hour is published without the record write

**Finding.** The first natural cron after deployment (15:00 UTC) wrote `report_failed` / `projector_window` although Discovery had been complete, with zero backlog, for hours. Read-only logs show the cause is a short real conflict, not a wrong write policy: the D1 enrichment projector holds the exclusive Discovery window (ADR 0103) for 36 to 148 s from about :00:42 every hour, and the close at about :00:49 reached its compare-and-swap of the executor record inside that window. A reconcile that answers `stopped` is read-only, so it never waited. The write gate was right to refuse; the close simply did not wait.

**Decision.** (1) `closeHourlyDiscoveryReport` waits for the window (`waitReconcileWindow`, bounded by the window's own deadline and the ADR 0100/AIR-428 cap) and re-reads the record before each of at most three tries; a competing checkpoint is therefore seen by the next read and its deltas are reported once. A normal report published after the wait is the exact-once report, with the original UTC hour and id. The fence is not weakened: no write enters the window. (2) If the window outlasts every try and the same consistent read proves the queue empty (`continuation.complete` of the same generation, `remaining` and `cheapRemaining` numeric zeros, no error, an accumulator with zero chunks and zero counters), the ordinary `kind=hour,status=empty` event with zero counters is published for the hour with `provisional: true`, without touching the record (the accumulator, outbox and `lastClosedHour` stay as they were). A later successful close of the same hour replaces the provisional event by compare-and-swap on its etag, so an hour has exactly one event; a provisional event never replaces a report. (3) Anything else keeps the notice: pending deltas, an unproven queue, a generation change, a lost compare-and-swap, an unreadable or missing record. The notice sentence no longer asserts that accumulated data exists («Если за этот период что-то было найдено, оно войдёт в следующий успешный отчёт»).

**Timing.** The event may appear up to a few minutes after HH:00 (the owner accepted a delay of about HH:05). HH:05 is not guaranteed: the wait is bounded by the window deadline, and the next hourly tick closes a still-open hour with `from`.

**Not decided here.** No new fallback beyond the proven-empty case; no cron or window change.

## Recovery and migration boundary

Code, tests and documents only. No production run, flag, schedule, lease, R2 or D1 write by an operator or event deletion is authorized; the cleanup of existing events needs its own owner GO.
