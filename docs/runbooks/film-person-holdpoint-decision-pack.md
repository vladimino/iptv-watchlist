Preflight: Discovery lease free, `discovery_transfer` not `loading`, no EPG run in flight (`sync.json` stage, executor control phase). - **Time zone:** state all times in Europe/Madrid (CET, UTC+1 in November) with UTC in parentheses in logs and evidence. A candidate slot is a few minutes after the Predictions run ends and ending before the next `:00` tick; the exact minutes must be read live from the lease rows, not assumed.

## H2: `prepared` to `open`, then the receipt baseline, then catch-up

**Status: BLOCKED, no GO.** The text below is design only.
**Would authorize (when unblocked):** one activation plan with bounded reads, the projector lease and exclusive window, a final empty projector pass, the single flip batch, readback, the receipt-baseline recording, the catch-up reconcile and the release; and no other writes.
**Preconditions:** all 30 blockers cleared (table above) **and** the extra items: held drill passed on a safe copy, mirror classification recorded, activation tooling and a receipt-baseline emitter reviewed and merged, H0 and H1 done and read back, a quiet window agreed in Madrid time, no pending/failed/unknown receipt (none can exist before `open`), every consumer verified on D1 (readers).
**Design sequence** (from ADR 0117 and the open handoff runbook; the tooling does not exist yet):
1. Bounded read-only preflight (counts only): marker `prepared`/`open`/1, fence ok, baseline `verified` with the recorded reference, schema 19, `canonical_revision` `COUNT(*), MAX(revision)` (expected 0; refused above the 5,000 cap), lease free, window idle, Discovery, EPG, Ratings and Predictions quiet, `handoffReadiness` `ready:true`.
2. Take the projector lease and the exclusive window (ADR 0103; required for H2 by E3); STOP if either is not granted.
3. Final projector pass with an empty plan; STOP if it plans any write.
4. **The flip, one atomic batch:** assert the lease is held and unexpired (and the window by the same owner); assert marker `prepared`/`open`/1; assert baseline `verified` with the expected reference; guarded `UPDATE film_person_authority` to `d1`/`open`/generation 2; the `film_person_cas` fence statement. All or nothing. Note the code gap: `transitionAuthority` does not assert lease, window or baseline, and the 0013 trigger `film_person_authority_needs_baseline` checks the baseline only when leaving `r2`, so a `prepared` to `d1` move is not baseline-checked in SQL; the batch must assert them itself (new reviewed code).
5. Readback: marker `d1`/`open`/2, fence ok, writer route `canonical`, the projector defers with `authority_unavailable` (ADR 0116).
6. **Receipt baseline, AFTER the flip** (the baseline tables accept inserts only at `d1`/`open`): `recordBaseline` copies `canonical_revision` in one fenced batch (a single SELECT, one snapshot) and inserts the watermark computed in SQL; then writes the seal; then `verifyBaseline` recomputes counts, highest revisions and digests read-only. Expected at the boundary: 0 entries, per-kind counts 0, digests of the empty set. Until the seal exists a receipt cannot be inserted, so producers stay refused (`receipt_baseline_missing`) by design. In the interval between steps 4 and 6 the quiet window is what keeps the producers parked; this interval is the period in which a canonical write with no baseline could otherwise happen, and it is not atomic with the flip.
7. Bounded catch-up reconcile of what producers could not write while `prepared`; settle `pending` and `unknown` receipts by readback and same-request-id replay.
8. Release the window and the lease. Mirror freeze and consumer verification (reads only).
**Read-back and expected values:** marker `d1`/`open`/2; `handoffReadiness` no longer evaluates (`marker_not_prepared:open`) but the receipt blockers read clean (`receipts_baseline_*` absent, `unreceipted_changes` and `receipts_attention_open` empty); watermark and seal present with equal digests; readers answer from D1 and no frozen mirror is read.
**Success:** all of the above and a window released inside its deadline.
**STOP:** any failed or ambiguous assertion; batch outcome unknown (read the marker, never retry blindly); a lease or window not ours; baseline not `verified` or a different reference; a planned write in step 3; a reader still on a frozen mirror; budget or time exceeded. On STOP the marker stays where it is and the evidence goes to the owner.
**Who:** Executor delivers the tooling and the drill; Controller runs the plan under the owner's GO; independent review of the tooling is mandatory before; the owner signs.
**Blast radius and risks, in plain language:**
- *What breaks if it goes wrong:* a marker moved without readers or producers on D1 stops or stales the product's film data paths. Readers fail closed (503) rather than fall back to R2 once the marker is not `prepared` or `r2`. A bad flip is repaired forward, not backward.
- *What is reversible:* `open` to `held` to `open`. Setting `mode=r2` is not allowed by the trigger from `d1`, and a git revert reverts neither the marker nor any D1 row.
- *What is not reversible:* every D1-only canonical write after `open`; the frozen R2 mirrors drift away from D1 from that point (old consumers go stale).
- *Time-of-check windows:* the catch-up is not atomic with the marker; the receipt baseline is a separate step after the flip; the lease and window cover them but are time-bounded.
**Not authorized:** H3, H4, any producer enabling outside the plan, any flag, schedule or cron change.

**Read-back and success:** the receipt is `applied` with the mutation in the same batch; `canonical_revision` advanced by exactly the planned amount; the differential shows only accepted differences; the projector remains deferred.
**STOP:** a receipt that is `pending`, `failed`, `unknown` or `abandoned`; an unclassified difference; any write outside the probe's target.
**Reversibility:** a real D1-only write; the remedy is a forward fix, not a revert. **Who:** Controller runs, owner signs.

## H4: held, recovery, rollback and any repair

**Status: BLOCKED, no GO; a GO per incident.**
**Would authorize, per case only:** moving `open` to `held` (readers answer 503 `film_person_held`, writers refuse, no reader names R2), the forward fix `held` to `open` (the trigger allows exactly that move, each by one generation), or a named repair. A blanket GO does not exist.
**Preconditions:** the held drill (open handoff runbook) passed on a safe copy: generation rises by exactly one per move; every writer attempt at `held` refused with no row changed; no reader names R2; a D1-only change survives `held` unchanged; after the forward fix the route is `canonical` and one fenced write succeeds; the trigger refuses `d1` to `r2`; a stale-generation batch fails whole; a reconcile pass runs after the forward fix (receipts left `pending` while `held` are frozen, not guessed). The drill harness is not shipped.
**Facts:** receipts `pending` at `held` stay frozen and settle after `open`; the projector is deferred at `d1` and `held`. A full reverse reconciler (rebuild R2 from D1, then `r2`) is **deferred**; once D1-only writes exist, `r2` and a git revert are not rollbacks (ADR 0117 decision 2, a refinement of ADR 0099 section 6). Code rollback reverts neither D1 data, the marker nor Access configuration.
**Who:** Controller executes, owner signs each step; an incident may need the owner within minutes, so the contact path and the decision rule belong in the plan.

## What stays NOT authorized by any of this

Any new flag, Scheduled Task or cron; any production read or write outside the holdpoint being signed; the reconciler's scheduling (a later separate GO); deleting or pruning receipts (append-only until a retention decision); freezing the programme and watchlist EPG mirrors, immutable sources and receipts, permitted input artifacts or the view-index retention (not frozen without an exact classification); republishing the legacy Site; a Time Travel restore; and any retry after an unknown outcome without reading the authoritative state first.
