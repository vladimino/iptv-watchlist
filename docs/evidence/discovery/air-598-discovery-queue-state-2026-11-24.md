## Root cause (code, `main` at `c5ecc1b`)

Five readers judged the same small records with their own rules:

| Reader | Rule before | Effect on the drained series |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Footer row «Распознавание» (`integrationHealth` in `src/enrichment.js`) | Did not read the Workflow record. A `chunk_budget` continuation went to the gate rule: once `nextCatchUpAt` plus 2 h 45 min had passed and no batch of the generation finished after it, any **ledger** count `readyNow > 0` made it `degraded` / `resolver_no_recent_progress`. | The ledger count of current unresolved observations is computed at read time over a sliding airing window; it was non-zero while the executor's own reading of the generation was 0. The row read as a stall with the Usage hint. |
| Monitor (`evaluateDiscovery` in `src/domain/monitor-policy.js`, facts from three separate reads in `src/monitor.js`) | `backlog = remainingChunks > 0 || stage running || reason chunk_budget`. | The drained continuation is a backlog; 3 h after the old gate it raises `discovery_stalled`. |
| Operational and detailed status (`discoveryExecutorReadout` in `src/discovery.js`) | `pending = remainingChunks > 0 || reason chunk_budget || running`; `eligible = decision.run || reason chunk_budget`; `degraded` includes "instance finished and pending". | `pending`, `eligible` and `degraded` all `true` for a finished, drained series (the 17:04 read above). |
| Events report (`discoveryReportQueue` in `src/discovery-workflow.js`) | Proved empty from `complete`, and since AIR-595 from the drained series (`discoveryDrainedSeries` + `discoveryDrainedStatus`). | Correct: «Очередь Discovery пуста». |
| Admin data health (`src/admin/health.js` + `src/domain/data-health.js`) | Read `epg/resolver-continuation.json` straight from R2, which is frozen since Discovery became D1-authoritative; `paused` with `remainingChunks 0` and no retry yielded nothing. | Judged a frozen record; the shortcut also hid a real `chunk_budget` backlog with `remainingChunks 0`. |

The execution policy is not part of the defect: a series whose last chunk drains the queue while the legacy alias sweep (ADR 0158) is open stops for good on `backlog_empty`, and nothing runs again for that generation until the next publication.

## Change

- `src/discovery.js`: one bounded reader `readDiscoveryEvidence` (the operational D1 snapshot, or the five small R2 objects outside D1 authority, plus `readDiscoveryGates` for the EPG-priority pause, read only) and one pure classification `discoveryQueueState` with the proof `discoveryQueueProof` (the AIR-595 drained proof and the `complete` proof, moved here from `src/discovery-workflow.js` so every reader shares them). States: `empty`, `running`, `backlog`, `paused`, `failed`, `blocked`, `unknown`; contract in the [Discovery queue state reference](../../reference/api/enrichment.md#discovery-queue-state).
- Operational status: adds `queue`; the detailed status adds `discoveryQueue` (from the records it already read); in both `executor.pending` is "not proven empty" and `executor.eligible` comes from the classification.
- Footer: the row maps the classification (`queue_empty` «очередь пуста · проверено …», `resolver_epg_priority`, `resolver_handoff`, `resolver_unconfirmed`); the ledger count no longer decides it.
- Monitor: facts from the shared reader; owed work follows the existing time rule as `discovery_stalled`; an unconfirmed state follows the same rule as the new `discovery_unconfirmed`; a proven empty queue and the pause raise nothing; a recovery says why (`queue_empty`, `epg_priority`), not progress.
- Events: `discoveryReportQueue` uses the shared proof; stored events are not rewritten.
- Admin data health: reads the continuation through the shared reader (the D1 record at D1 authority) and judges with the attached classification.

No new persisted state, authority, writer, threshold, schedule or flag. Thresholds (3 h, gate grace 2 h 45 min) are unchanged. Not an ADR: an existing source and existing proof rules are reused; the classification is computed on every read.

## Independent review follow-up (24 Nov 2026)

The independent review of [vladimino/iptv-watchlist#984](https://github.com/vladimino/iptv-watchlist/pull/984#issuecomment-7139653650) on HEAD `a1964dfcb6e0` asked for four changes, all made in the same PR:

- **P1, a strict `complete` proof.** The first version proved a `complete` continuation with the permissive event predicate (remaining and cheap-pass zero, `!(waitingRetry > 0)`), so `deferred: 7`, a missing or negative waiting-retry count, or a zero status of another hop passed as empty, and the classification returned `empty` before a quarantine or a live writer was seen. Now both proofs need the status of the continuation's own hop with all four parts the number 0 and no error, no quarantined executor record and no live Discovery writer (the lease read with its holder kind; only the EPG publication's lease does not count; a lease that could not be read proves nothing). The Events report reads that status and the lease too; the period's accumulated reading is shown, never a proof. The permissive rule survives only for legacy per-chain events, under its own name.
- **P1, the admission gate fails closed.** A gate that answers `paused: null`, reports itself unreadable or cannot be read made owed work eligible. Now the gate is kept whole with the published generation its read saw; owed work under an unreadable gate is `unknown` / `admission_unreadable` and under a gate of another generation (a publication between the two reads) `unknown` / `gate_generation_moved`, never eligible. A D1 reader without a gate read is unreadable, not clear; the detailed status uses the same gate reader.
- **P1, no silent recovery.** A missing continuation or an unconfirmed state made the Discovery stream healthy, and the open `discovery_stalled` was recovered without proof. Now an unknown state runs on its own clock (`unknownSince`, stored in `monitor/state.json`), is `waiting` (an expected handoff) or `delayed` (missing proof) and becomes `discovery_unconfirmed` after 3 hours; an open incident is held under its own key with its last measured metrics and a typed `held` reason through unreadable or unconfirmed ticks, a changed diagnosis stays under the open key with `transition`, and it recovers once only on `queue_empty`, `epg_priority` or `within_policy`.
- **P2, timing from the published generation.** The Monitor combined the raw continuation gate and time with the raw status finish time, whatever their generation, and a future `chunk_budget` gate made a renewed backlog wait. Now the rule reads `allowedAt`, `checkpointAt` and `lastChunkAt` of the classification only; a known budget the Workflow renews at once is allowed from its checkpoint.

Thresholds are unchanged. Tests: `tests/discovery-queue-state.mjs` adds complete-path negatives (unknown waiting-retry, deferred films, another hop), a live Discovery writer and the EPG publication's lease on the drained records, the D1 gate answering unknown, unreadable, throwing, absent from the reader and of another generation (operational, footer and the detailed status's gate reader), a missing continuation after a sent alert (held for four ticks, then a diagnosis transition under the same key, an unreadable tick held, one recovery on the proof), repeated unknown snapshots (`delayed` three ticks, then `discovery_unconfirmed` once), a recent batch of another generation that does not hide a stall, and a future leftover gate that does not wait a renewed backlog. `tests/discovery-events.mjs` adds the complete-path negatives on the Events report and the strict zero reading; `tests/monitor-policy.mjs` and `tests/monitor-message.mjs` cover the clock, the hold, the transition and the recovery texts. Fixtures of Monitor tests now carry a finished Discovery series of the published generation, as production has.

## Second review follow-up (24 Nov 2026)

The [re-review](https://github.com/vladimino/iptv-watchlist/pull/984#issuecomment-7140514510) of `9cbf48e` accepted the strict proof and the generation-valid timing and asked for two more changes, both made in the same PR:

- **P1, admission on every new writer.** An orphaned `running` record (lease absent or expired) and a recorded error with a due retry returned `eligible: true` before the admission check, and a `running` checkpoint under the EPG publication's live lease was taken for a live Discovery writer. Now resuming an orphan, retrying an error and running owed work all go through the same admission: a held pause is `paused`, an unreadable gate `unknown` / `admission_unreadable`, a gate of another generation `unknown` / `gate_generation_moved`, each with `eligible` not true, no `allowedAt` and the underlying `diagnosis` kept; the running branch uses the Discovery-writer predicate, so the EPG lease leaves an orphan.
- **P1, no recovery from uncertainty.** An unread lease was `running` (a writer) and owed work without any usable time was healthy by the time rule, so a sent incident could recover `within_policy`. Now an unread lease is `unknown` / `lease_unread`; owed work with no allowed time, checkpoint or finished chunk of this generation runs on the unconfirmed clock (`metrics.timing: unknown`); `within_policy` needs owed work measured with real times. Independent critical issues (quarantine) stay visible.

The Monitor keeps one new field in its own state document, `monitor/state.json`: `discovery.unknownSince`, written by the hourly Monitor tick together with the rest of that document; every read route stays read-only. Tests: the cross-product of an orphaned record, an orphan under the EPG lease and a due error with no gate, a clear, paused, unreadable and moved admission (state, reason, eligibility, diagnosis, no allowed time), the operational status and the footer under a refused gate, and a Monitor runtime run where a sent stall is held through two ticks of an unread lease and two ticks of untimed owed work, then recovers once on measured progress.
