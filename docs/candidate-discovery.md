# Candidate discovery

[AIR-80](https://trello.com/c/hQPjVTXv) adds search variants for an EPG title. A search hit is not a verified decision by itself. [AIR-95](https://trello.com/c/kD8xjvVu) lets the scorer treat one reversible form as title evidence: a numeral word, a trailing `1` removed from the title, or the single `е`/`э` swap the variant generator already produces. The movie title or original title must equal that form exactly. The detail gate repeats the same check. A prefix, a trailing `2` reduced to its stem, and a `tmdb_alias` still are not title evidence.

`CANDIDATE_DISCOVERY` accepts only `shadow` or `on`. Unset and any other text are off. The repository does not commit the variable. Production leaves it unset. [AIR-81](https://trello.com/c/ywuOtfo0) planned the extra search and skipped it; the record is `docs/air-81-parity.md`. [AIR-84](https://trello.com/c/E7wlg7uV) sent the searches on a later generation and recorded INSUFFICIENT DATA in `docs/air-84-shadow.md`. The flag was removed again after that sample. [AIR-86](https://trello.com/c/INQy9Hca) counted every stored `retryAt` in `docs/air-86-census.md`, then replayed 38 of those rows without writing production. The result is `docs/air-86-replay.md`: 2 of 38 reached `detail_verified`. Production leaves `CANDIDATE_DISCOVERY` unset. [AIR-68](https://trello.com/c/qbLDc6Ql) stays blocked.

`shadow` pairs with `SCORED_RESOLUTION=shadow`. The extra hits update the preview only. The legacy decision and the catalog stay as the legacy branch wrote them. `on` pairs with `SCORED_RESOLUTION=on` and feeds the same hits to the existing scored writer, which still verifies only after `detail_verified`. An exact title found at the neighbouring year can. A reversible structural form can too, after the detail card repeats that form, the id, the year, and the runtime. A `tmdb_alias` proposal is not passed to the scorer. Setting only one of the two variables does not activate the search, and the legacy `else` branch never calls it.

Rollback of the extra search is unsetting `CANDIDATE_DISCOVERY`. Authoritative rows do not need a rewrite.

The extra search uses `tmdbRequest`. It does not increment `tmdbCalls`. The chunk result and `epg/discovery-status.json` record `variantCalls`, `variantErrors`, `variantThrottled`, `variantSkipped`, and `variantDeferred` separately. One observation may plan 4 search queries, then up to 3 movie-detail reads for alternative titles, then one verification read when the score is already `accepted`. That is at most 8 optional TMDB requests, and the chunk cap is also 8, so one observation can use the whole optional budget. A query starts only when more than 12 seconds remain. A shadow plan that cannot start, or that stops before its queries and alias reads are finished, is stored in `epg/discovery-variant-pending.json` for the same `programmeRunId`, with the decision from before the legacy write and the evidence fingerprint. A later resolve continues that plan. The comparison uses that saved eligibility, and it still requires the observation to be current, the evidence fingerprint to match, the decision not to be terminal, and the published generation to match. It does not clear `retryAt` and it does not rewrite the legacy decision or the catalog. The preview can still reach `detail_verified`. `variantDeferred` counts stored plans, not finished searches. A changed published generation drops them. The `on` writer does not read or write this file. If its optional search cannot finish, it does not record `retryAt`, so the observation stays on the normal queue and the writer can verify it later. A stored shadow plan keeps the proposals and search hits already found, so a later resolve can verify that same id when the movie-detail read was the part that did not fit. The chunk reports that plan as `variantPending`. It is not added to `remaining`. While it is non-zero, the continuation stays `continue` inside the current chunk budget, or `wait` when that budget is spent. It becomes `stop` only after the plan is finished. A generation change and an HTTP 429 still stop optional work. The first HTTP 429 sets `variantThrottled` and stops every later optional request in the chunk; those unsent requests are `variantSkipped` and are not stored. A timeout or other error is counted in `variantErrors` and does not stop the later variants. A missing year (`null`, absent, or `''`) does not become year 0.

When several search rows fail the title check, up to three are ordered by year distance, then runtime distance, then shared title tokens, and each is read for translations and alternative titles while the attempt cap remains. The matching id is recorded as `tmdb_alias`. It is not passed to the scorer as an alias, so it stays a proposal.

Variants, in order:

| Reason | Query |
| --- | --- |
| `part_suffix` | Drop a trailing `1` or `2` when the stem contains a letter. `1984` and `7500` have no stem to drop. |
| `spelling_e` | On a single token of at most eight characters, swap `е` and `э`. `Блейд` and `Блэйд` meet. A hyphenated or multi-word title does not. |
| `numeral` | A leading `1`–`12` followed by Cyrillic becomes the Russian word. `11 друзей Оушена` becomes `одиннадцать друзей Оушена`. |
| `year_before`, `year_after` | The original title at one year either side. |

A hit is kept when its year is within one of the observation, and its title equals the variant, equals the observation, or one title extends the other by another token. Only an exact title match on `part_suffix`, `spelling_e`, or `numeral` is attached as structural evidence. The scorer recomputes that form from the observation and keeps every distinct valid claim, including when the exact title arrives in a later chunk for an id already stored. The detail competitor list includes films indexed under that form as well as under the EPG title. `Бегущий человек` in 1987 does not keep a 2025 edition. The preview field `proposedCandidates` records the id, query, year, and reason.

If no hit matches, up to three year-compatible rows are ordered by year distance, then runtime distance, then shared title tokens. Each is read for translations and alternative titles while the chunk cap remains. A matching title is recorded as `tmdb_alias` and is not given to the scorer. The id is a proposal. `Мортал Комбат` stays `no_confident_match` even when it is not the first search row. A longer title such as `Чудо-женщина: 1984` is a proposal for `Чудо-женщина`, not an acceptance.

An exact title at the neighbouring year, such as `Ирония судьбы` in 1976, is a real scorer candidate. Shadow may then show `detail_verified` on the preview. The stored decision stays the legacy `no_confident_match`, and no catalog row is created.
