## Uncertainty and limits

- Synthetic shape only: two shows per film and per candidate, 2,300 films. The real generation's show distribution was not read; ADR-0021's GO is for this size, and a markedly larger real generation should be measured again.
- One run, one time of day, one client location. Timings are a single sample, not a distribution over days.
- The scratch database now holds the `g4-prior` (superseded) and `g4-rival` (failed) staging rows and the committed synthetic generations; discarding them needs the separate `--cleanup` approval. A second `--run` on this database returns `INCOMPLETE` by design.
- Discard in bounded batches (part of the ADR-0021 scratch plan) was not exercised because `--cleanup` was not approved.
- Not tested: production data, the database `iptv`, the backfill executor's other units, Worker bindings.
